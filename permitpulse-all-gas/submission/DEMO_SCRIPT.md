# PermitPulse — 2:45 demo script

**0:00–0:15 — Problem + product.** “Permit and inspection guidance changes quietly. PermitPulse keeps an evidence trail instead of turning a crawl into unqualified legal advice.” Open the product home and one jurisdiction.

**0:15–0:45 — Convex is the spine.** Show persisted jurisdiction/source state, a prior snapshot, the new snapshot, and owner-review checklist status. Click through a live update so judges can see this is stateful Convex work, not a static frontend.

**0:45–1:15 — Firecrawl does real work.** Run one admitted live source refresh. Show the requested official URL, provider-returned source identity, digest, and detected text change. If the provider call is unavailable, do not substitute the synthetic fixture in the final video.

**1:15–1:40 — AgentMail does real work.** Show one real inbound notice entering the jurisdiction inbox and the event/message/thread dedupe identity. PermitPulse is inbound-only; do not send an outbound email for the demo.

**1:40–2:10 — OpenAI does bounded work.** Generate operational review steps from the documented change. Show citations/source linkage and the `owner-review-required` state. State plainly that this is review assistance, not legal advice or autonomous compliance action.

**2:10–2:30 — Fail closed.** Demonstrate one source-identity mismatch or duplicate inbound event being rejected. Briefly say that the repository’s synthetic fixture is a contract test and never counts as live provider evidence.

**2:30–2:45 — Close.** Show the public app URL, public GitHub repo, root `hackathon.md`, and the operator flow: monitor → inspect evidence → review checklist → act outside PermitPulse under human authority.

Final recording rule: keep the actual video under 180 seconds. Prefer clicks over narration. Do not record fake provider receipts.
