# CivTech 12.3 — CircularValue exploration packet

## Product hypothesis

CircularValue makes wider circular-practice value inspectable instead of collapsing it into one headline number. Each lever is linked to a retained evidence artifact, confidence class, bounded value range and evidence date.

The prototype separates direct cash effects from supply-chain resilience, resource security, customer retention and market-shock exposure. It reports which uncertainty dominates the scenario and whether the entered case is robust across the full range or depends on optimistic assumptions.

## Exploration questions

- Which wider-benefit categories are useful to Scottish SMEs and decision makers?
- Which source artifacts can be captured with low burden from existing business systems?
- Which effects should remain qualitative rather than monetized?
- Which evidence-age and confidence conventions improve trust?
- Which aggregate insights could support Zero Waste Scotland without exposing company-level data?

## Source proof

Strict JSON, integer minor units, bounded basis points, exact source and evidence SHA-256 custody, deterministic receipts, replay verification, stale-evidence routing and post-import verifier-policy rebinding hostiles are included.

No live company data, sponsor validation, application submission, award, contract or payment is claimed by the prototype.
