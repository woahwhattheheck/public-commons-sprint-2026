Geographic and model meaning

Whole HUC12 planning footprints follow retained ToHUC links to the issuer-selected anchors. Published intake protection polygons retain a separate source and geometry.

USGS whole-HUC fields, NRCS whole-HUC acres and NRCS state-portion acres preserve their original editions and rounding. Each HUC is counted once in a union; state rows are summed once per state/HUC.

InVEST 3.20 NDR/SDR estimate land-origin export reaching the modeled stream network. HUC relationships associate that land with the selected receptor. In-stream retention, reservoir processes and intake concentrations require additional modeling.
