L0044@PDF04: A. Criteria for Classes.
L0045@PDF04: All adopted classifications must conform to the standards and rules contained within R.61-68, Water
L0046@PDF04: Classifications and Standards, or site-specific standards listed within this regulation. Unless noted,
L0047@PDF04: site- specific standards apply only to the water named and not to tributary or downstream waters.
L0048@PDF04: B. Tributaries to Classified Waters.
L0049@PDF04: Where surface waters are not classified by name (unlisted) in this regulation, the use classification and
L0050@PDF04: numeric standards of the class of the stream to which they are tributary apply, disregarding any site-specific
L0051@PDF04: numeric standards for the named waterbody. In tidal areas, where an unlisted tributary may affect or flows
L0052@PDF04: between two differently classified waterbodies, regardless of whether the location is upstream or
L0053@PDF04: downstream, the more stringent numeric standards of the classified waters apply to the unlisted tributary,
L0054@PDF04: disregarding any site-specific numeric standards for those waterbodies.
L0055@PDF04: C. Status of Classifications and Reviews.
L0056@PDF04: The classification for all bodies of water contained herein supersedes all previous classifications. The
L0057@PDF04: classifications listed within this regulation shall be open to review to ensure that the classification use is
L0058@PDF04: still valid and justified.
L0059@PDF04: D. No Discharge Zone Designations.
L0060@PDF04: The Department may determine in accordance with Section 312 of the Clean Water Act that for some
L0061@PDF04: waterbodies (or portions of waterbodies), the designation of No Discharge Zone (NDZ) for Marine
L0062@PDF04: Sanitation Devices (MSDs) shall be enacted with application of the existing classified standards of the
L0063@PDF04: waterbody. The designation is listed in this regulation as an NDZ following the waterbody name.
