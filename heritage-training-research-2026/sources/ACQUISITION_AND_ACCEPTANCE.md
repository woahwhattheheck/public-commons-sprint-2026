## Complete acceptance

The engineering handover is accepted when the existing operator can:

- Open every selected original file and inspect a mapped record's exact original row or locator.
- Follow a real qualification through its source/version, learning-aim mapping, dated funding record and content review.
- Distinguish standard-level evidence from an option and a provider identity from its delivery sites.
- See raw disclosure tokens and their source-specific meanings in both the UI and exports.
- Select comparable populations and nonoverlapping partitions, reproduce a saved analysis, and inspect why a requested aggregation is unsuitable.
- Export the complete source/working package, receive it independently, and reproduce the saved output without fetching a new edition silently.
- Add a following source edition while retaining earlier research decisions, analysis results and source lineage.

The research handover additionally needs a signed-off scope, traceable content decisions, explicit coverage/response denominators and the lead's interpretation of gaps and risk. These are completed research deliverables, not quantities invented by the application. The workbench can finish a useful partial-coverage baseline when its coverage is stated precisely.
