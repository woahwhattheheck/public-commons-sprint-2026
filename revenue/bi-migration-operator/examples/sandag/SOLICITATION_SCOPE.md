## Scope

| Item | Original requirement |
|---|---|
| Term and estimated range | Three years; $300,000–$360,000; local funding. |
| Existing estate | About 1,500 reports, 12 agency customers, 500 Cognos users, three Framework Manager models over SQL Server. |
| First delivery platform | On-premises Power BI Report Server. Recreate formatted reports, reviewed SQL/model mappings and equivalent outputs with customer accuracy/performance acceptance. |
| Continuing service | Cognos operations and support throughout the term; response to critical helpdesk issues within 48 hours; 10–15 recorded training hours for more than 100 users in the final two years. |
| Later platform | Azure/cloud migration from 2027, with 12-agency usage and cost allocation/bill-back. |
| Proposed team | Project Manager, Data Architect, Senior Analyst and Analyst. Similar law-enforcement experience, Cognos/Power BI/SQL skills and external Python processes are evaluated. |
| Access | Applicable CJIS/CLETS checks, approved fingerprint clearance before logical/physical ARJIS access, and continuing compliance/training. A qualified prime supplies the actual approved staffing and access arrangements. |
