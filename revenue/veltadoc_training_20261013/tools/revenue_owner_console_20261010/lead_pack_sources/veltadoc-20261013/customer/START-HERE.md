## Start a useful conversation

1. Read the provider's linked course and contact pages before approaching it.
2. Refer to the specific certificate that provider already offers.
3. Ask whether preparing a batch from an approved class list would be useful. The provider's current software and preparation time are discovery questions.
4. Offer a small demonstration using fictional attendees. Keep the provider's approved certificate wording, logo and signature authority.
5. Let the course administrator confirm attendance or completion before generating the real batch. Review names, course title, date, layout and every output page before printing or delivery.

The proposed workflow uses the provider's own participation, attendance or completion document. Where a school also offers an external qualification, keep the awarding body's issuing process with that body. For Photoion, work through school administration within its current City Academy affiliation. Shoreditch's PDF attendance certificate and its digital badge are separate outputs.
