# Configured permitting cutover workshare

**Proposed delivery:** $4,800 fixed · ten business days after agreed inputs · 50% kickoff / 50% acceptance.

TokenJunkie Labs can provide a configured acceptance workspace and hands-on reconciliation for one permitting implementation cohort. The implementation partner supplies its approved source and candidate exports and retains the customer relationship. We carry the selected population through original-file intake, explicit mapping, case review, corrected reruns and a recoverable operator handoff.

## The delivery

The first engagement covers one source-to-target export family, up to 2,500 selected case records including their in-cohort relationships, and two corrected candidate exports. The cohort may include permits, licenses, inspections or a separately timed special-event rollout. The platform owner agrees the population and observation point before capture.

We configure original field and case-ID mappings; department and status equivalences; departmental review states, assignments and due dates; attachment counts; and the agreed case-level payment field. The retained workspace shows exact case discrepancies with original file/row context and records named decisions. Corrected runs preserve earlier reviews and isolate the remaining work.

We configure the applicable departmental acceptance scenarios with their actual names and statuses. Berkeley's published Building/Fire finalization dependency and open-review ownership/due-date requirements are working examples already supported in the application. The partner's reviewers observe the actual lifecycle and record their decisions alongside the candidate snapshot results.

The handoff includes the working application source, configuration, exact source-linked findings, selected-run decisions and history, private JSON/CSV outputs, a complete recoverable workspace and an operator walkthrough. The partner can continue reruns with the supplied source.

## Inputs and responsibilities

| Partner supplies | We deliver |
| --- | --- |
| A named technical owner and the agreed cohort, capture point and extraction scope | A retained intake configuration and an explicit included/excluded population record |
| Original source and candidate CSV/JSON reports, stable IDs or an authoritative one-to-one crosswalk | Field/relationship mapping, normalization preview and actionable original-row errors |
| Department/status/reviewer vocabulary and the relevant lifecycle requirements | Configured comparison and acceptance scenarios with exact case drilldown |
| Reviewer decisions and the corrected candidate captures | Prior-review continuity, two corrected reruns and the remaining exception queue |
| The selected reviewers' real lifecycle observations and handoff recipient | The selected cutover decision record, recoverable workspace and operator transfer |

Exports stay in the agreed local or partner-controlled environment. We will agree any access, handling and procurement requirements in the engagement. The supplied application runs with Python and Node standard libraries and can be operated entirely from original report exports.

## Delivery sequence

**Days 1–2:** Agree the acceptance population, capture point and column contract. Load the original reports, configure mappings and crosswalks, and resolve input errors with the source owner.

**Days 3–5:** Reconcile the selected cases through the original comparator. Review exact discrepancies, configure departmental scenarios and return a correction queue with case and original-file references.

**Days 6–8:** Process up to two corrected candidate exports, preserve review continuity and reconcile the remaining differences with the named reviewers.

**Days 9–10:** Complete the selected-run decision record, transfer source/configuration/outputs and demonstrate backup recovery and repeat operation with the recipient.

The start date follows receipt of the agreed exports, vocabulary and reviewer availability. Additional export families, ongoing live API synchronization, document-content comparison and daily transaction-level financial reconciliation can be priced as distinct follow-on deliverables from their actual data contracts.

## Acceptance

The partner can reopen the supplied workspace; trace each selected case and finding to original bytes and mappings; inspect and update decisions; run the agreed corrected candidates; inspect the configured scenario results; and export the selected handoff. Every required current-run discrepancy and scenario has a named disposition. The partner retains the final decision to accept the actual system cutover.
