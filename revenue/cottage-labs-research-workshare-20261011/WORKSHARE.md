# Open research data implementation workshare

**Prepared for Cottage Labs · 11 October 2026**

## Delivery

**USD 6,400 over ten business days**, starting after the agreed project inputs and delivery window. The proposed milestones are **USD 3,200 at kickoff** and **USD 3,200 at accepted implementation and handover**.

We propose a complete implementation arm alongside Cottage Labs' institutional research and open-access projects: configured source acquisition, a retained research-review workflow, a useful service/output integration and a working recipient handover. The original application preserves the query, received metadata and decisions so a researcher can revisit an edition as the live catalogue changes.

Cottage Labs' [NGLP project](https://cottagelabs.com/projects/nglp/) describes connectors for DSpace/OJS and shared usage/workflow reporting. Its [repository services](https://cottagelabs.com/data-repositories/) include custom metadata profiles, workflows and integrations. This proposed workshare applies our maintained OpenAlex research application to one agreed customer use case.

## Included operating cohort

| Component | Included |
|---|---|
| Research scope | Two approved OpenAlex works queries, up to 2,500 identified records each |
| Retained editions | Two captures per query: an initial edition and a later comparison |
| Environment | One customer-controlled deployment |
| Integration | One agreed metadata-service route or CSV/JSON research output interface |
| Review | Two consolidated review/correction cycles |
| Handover | Working full-edition receiver, original source, reports and a 90-minute engineer/operator session |
| Defect correction | Fourteen calendar days after accepted delivery |

The query scope and account budget are agreed before acquisition. A query with a larger matching population is refined with the researcher or receives an agreed expanded scope. The application displays actual retained membership, provider-reported totals and cursor progress together.

## Complete implementation

1. Configure two approved OpenAlex works queries for a named research or library use case; agree fields, filters, source period, account budget and publication permissions.

2. Install the retained researcher workspace in one customer-controlled environment, with original query revisions, complete cursor/page records and source coverage.

3. Set up useful record review, notes and selections; author/institution/topic/funder summaries; supplied-field and duplicate reporting; and a later-capture comparison.

4. Integrate one reviewed metadata-service route or agreed CSV/JSON research output interface using the existing maintained operator/client workflow.

5. Deliver the exact source pages, complete selected research editions, original review history and reports, bibliography/CSV/JSON outputs, the working receiver and a newly reopened private workspace.

6. Complete two consolidated review/correction cycles, one 90-minute engineer/operator handover and a written operating and recovery guide.

The receiver validates the original edition manifest and every included file. It installs the original query, selected revision, edition, pages, native result records and review decisions into a new private workspace, then opens that workspace through the same research application. Repeat receiving preserves later reviewer edits.

The source contains a maintained OpenAlex operator, original merchant/client integration and retained research workspace. Open metadata retains its source license; linked full-text documents retain their own rights. The configured delivery route and any payment settings are explicit customer choices.

## Customer inputs and working schedule

- A named project owner and researcher/reviewer, the two research questions and what useful output will be accepted.
- Approved OpenAlex query or institutional identifiers, selected fields/date range and an agreed record envelope; the customer's provider account if authenticated acquisition is selected.
- One Linux/Node deployment environment with appropriate private storage and the customer-controlled service or output destination.
- The selected access and delivery model; any native merchant terms and signer/facilitator configuration stay with the authorized customer/operator.
- Consolidated reviewer feedback within one business day during the agreed delivery window.

| Delivery period | Working result |
|---|---|
| Days 1–2 | Approved queries, source/account setup, field and acceptance decisions |
| Days 3–5 | Initial acquisition, research workspace and selected service/output integration |
| Days 6–8 | Research review, later capture, comparison and first correction cycle |
| Days 9–10 | Final correction, receiving/reopen, acceptance and operator handover |

The schedule assumes one-business-day consolidated reviews. Source/API entitlement, unusually large metadata records and customer infrastructure are reviewed at kickoff; agreed changes are recorded against the same implementation scope.

## Acceptance

- Each source request and received page can be traced to the selected query revision; coverage, missing fields, duplicates and any interrupted acquisition remain visible.
- Review decisions and useful research outputs survive restart; the later edition comparison retains the original IDs and before/after metadata.
- An engineer receives the complete exported edition into a new private workspace and reopens the original query, page bytes and review decisions.
- The agreed customer-facing service/output works with the designated recipient and the operator can repeat the documented refresh, review, export and recovery procedure.

Handover includes the maintained source and dependency instructions; reviewed configuration; saved queries and complete selected editions; source and coverage metadata; original decisions; reports and bibliographies; and the receiving/recovery instructions. Customer credentials remain in the customer-controlled runtime.

The fee includes fourteen calendar days of correction for defects in the delivered implementation. Initial response is within two business days, followed by diagnosis and an agreed correction date.

Optional care is **USD 600 per month for four engineering hours** covering the delivered configuration, source-field changes and a monthly retained-edition/recovery review. Additional approved time is **USD 150 per hour**.

Provider charges, hosting and third-party subscriptions stay in the customer-approved account.
