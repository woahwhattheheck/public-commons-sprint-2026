# VentCRM operator commissioning desk

## Use the actual business and one continuous operating course

This packet supplies the source-linked criteria for the existing commissioning product. Start with the operator's original export and keep the same customer, scheduled work, invoice and communications connected through correction, receiving and handover. Each criterion below records useful operating work inside that course; it is not an instruction to create a separate automated test suite.

The current public policy pages all show an August 13, 2026 draft notice. Preserve that status alongside the operator's selected agreement. The current published service describes a small route-business setup; the prepared desk should carry the actual selected scope and pricing, including a negotiated successor when applicable. See the [pricing](https://ventcrm.com/pricing), [terms](https://ventcrm.com/terms) and [migration](https://ventcrm.com/jobber-alternative) sources.

## Daily exports and the complete handover

Maintain two distinct received-file lists. The first records outputs actually downloaded from the ordinary export views. The second maps the complete agreed handover and identifies how each missing object or relationship will be delivered. Retain original files, record counts, key mappings and the result of opening the same records in a clean destination. The [export policy](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation) distinguishes these delivery scopes.

## Record decisions where the operator works

Use the supplied JSON template as the initial case. The commissioning app may retain its own field names while preserving the facts and relationships. Status values are unreviewed, complete, needs_work and not_in_scope. A completed item records who did the work, when, what happened and the actual output. A scope exclusion records its operator decision. Open work carries a concrete owner and needed output; do not invent a provider capability or agreement to fill a blank.

The desk should offer a readable summary, a full editable JSON export and the original source/file register. Reopening the JSON must preserve criterion identities, selected agreements, original file identities, correction history and open work. Store credentials in their established account custody; the desk needs only nonsecret references.

## Complete operating criteria

### scope: Select one actual operating business

**Phase:** intake.

Record business identity, service types, crew size, operator contact, operating timezone and its current system. List required integrations and any payroll or inventory dependency.

**Completed outcome:** The selected service matches the operator's actual daily route; each unusual dependency has a named treatment in the agreed scope.

**Retain:** Business profile, dependency list and selected scope.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/); [Acceptable use](https://ventcrm.com/acceptable-use).

### terms: Keep published terms and the selected agreement distinct

**Phase:** intake.

Record links, retrieval dates and draft labels beside the agreement actually selected for this pilot. Record who selected it and when setup work begins.

**Completed outcome:** The desk can display the source version separately from the operator's agreed scope, dates and commercial treatment. An unresolved source detail stays visible as a question assigned to its actual owner.

**Retain:** Source register, agreement reference, setup-start event and open questions.

**Published basis:** [Terms of service](https://ventcrm.com/terms); [Cancellation and data export](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation).

### fees: Record the actual service and usage basis

**Phase:** intake.

Enter the selected setup/monthly amounts, billing start, usage responsibility and payment-account owner. Preserve a later negotiated amount beside the published reference.

**Completed outcome:** The operating budget separates software, SMS and card costs and identifies whose account receives customer payments.

**Retain:** Selected commercial record and usage-cost owner.

**Published basis:** [Pricing and setup](https://ventcrm.com/pricing).

### identity: Configure the operator's identity and service catalogue

**Phase:** setup.

Load the agreed name, logo, domain, services, prices, taxes, service area and booking rules from the intake.

**Completed outcome:** One authored booking displays the selected operator identity and service details consistently through the invoice and customer document.

**Retain:** Configuration export and the authored booking reference.

**Published basis:** [Pricing and setup](https://ventcrm.com/pricing).

### source: Retain the original export before mapping

**Phase:** migration.

Receive the operator-authorized original files, inventory every file and count records by entity. Preserve original bytes and timestamps before any transformations.

**Completed outcome:** Every received file remains available and is attributable to one intake; corrections are separate editions with a stated reason.

**Retain:** Original-file manifest, row counts and intake owner.

**Published basis:** [Jobber migration and cutover](https://ventcrm.com/jobber-alternative).

### mapping: Make each field and relationship explicit

**Phase:** migration.

Map source identifiers, customer/property relationships, visits, invoice amounts/statuses and dates. Record timezone, currency and duplicate rules. Keep exceptions assigned to an operator.

**Completed outcome:** Every in-scope source field is mapped, intentionally retained as extra information or explicitly excluded; customer/job/invoice references remain connected.

**Retain:** Complete mapping, duplicate decisions and unresolved-source list.

**Published basis:** [Jobber migration and cutover](https://ventcrm.com/jobber-alternative).

### balances: Reconcile operational and financial continuity

**Phase:** migration.

Compare imported counts, future appointments, outstanding balances and paid/void states to the original export. Resolve missing links and unexpected differences within the same case.

**Completed outcome:** Booked work and invoice balances can be traced to the received source; every remaining difference has a documented operator decision.

**Retain:** Before/after reconciliation and the complete exception decisions.

**Published basis:** [Jobber migration and cutover](https://ventcrm.com/jobber-alternative).

### delta: Plan the changeover from the still-running system

**Phase:** migration.

Choose a cutover window, identify records created or changed during preparation, and name the responsible operator for the final delta and fallback.

**Completed outcome:** The plan accounts for new bookings and payments through the selected switch time, with a way to resume the original process if the cutover is deferred.

**Retain:** Cutover worksheet, delta inventory and fallback contact.

**Published basis:** [Jobber migration and cutover](https://ventcrm.com/jobber-alternative).

### owner_access: Give the operator an individual account

**Phase:** access.

Use the intended owner identity in the selected private instance. Exercise ordinary sign-in, sign-out and the supported recovery path during the walkthrough.

**Completed outcome:** The owner can return to the intended business workspace through its normal entry point; credentials are kept out of the desk and shared documents.

**Retain:** Instance URL, named account custodian and walkthrough result.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### technician_access: Review the actual technician role

**Phase:** access.

With an operator-authorized technician account, open its normal day view and one same-day invoice. In the same workflow inspect the owner-only routes the operator depends on.

**Completed outcome:** The observed role fits the published daily-work boundary; any broader access is recorded against the exact route for the product owner to correct.

**Retain:** Role matrix and actual route/result notes.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### custody: Record system and provider custody

**Phase:** access.

Name the business owner and technical custodian of the instance, domain, data store, email, messaging and payment connections. Record references and recovery contacts without secrets.

**Completed outcome:** The operator can identify who controls each connection and where customer data belongs; a handover does not require a password embedded in a document.

**Retain:** Account-custody register and recovery contacts.

**Published basis:** [Privacy policy](https://ventcrm.com/privacy).

### booking: Operate a booking through the selected rules

**Phase:** daily operation.

Use an authored pilot customer and the configured public booking route; carry the booking into the schedule, visit and final invoice on the operator's phone.

**Completed outcome:** The intended service, time, address and selected charges remain consistent through the operator's normal day.

**Retain:** One linked booking-to-invoice course and reviewed phone/desktop views.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### consent: Carry consent with the contact

**Phase:** communications.

For controlled pilot contacts, record consent source, scope, wording/version and time. Make absent or limited consent visible before enabling communications.

**Completed outcome:** Only the communication appropriate to the recorded scope is selected; a service contact is not silently reclassified as a marketing subscriber.

**Retain:** Contact permission record and selected communication purpose.

**Published basis:** [SMS policy](https://ventcrm.com/sms-policy).

### registration: Join the actual business messaging connection

**Phase:** communications.

Retain the provider's actual number/registration result and responsible custodian. Use a consenting operator-controlled contact for a send and reply in the private pilot instance.

**Completed outcome:** The chosen business sender and reply appear in the intended conversation; registration status is an observed provider result.

**Retain:** Nonsecret registration reference, sender identity and message result.

**Published basis:** [SMS policy](https://ventcrm.com/sms-policy).

### optout: Continue the same conversation through an opt-out

**Phase:** communications.

Use the controlled contact's opt-out in the selected pilot course, then inspect its recorded communication preference before the next queued or manual action.

**Completed outcome:** The contact's new preference is retained and reflected in subsequent messaging decisions; the course records the actual observed result.

**Retain:** Opt-out event and subsequent communication disposition.

**Published basis:** [SMS policy](https://ventcrm.com/sms-policy).

### automation: Select automation with the operator

**Phase:** communications.

Identify the actual triggers enabled for the authored course, their recipient, timing and owner. Review the resulting customer copy before a selected trigger is exercised.

**Completed outcome:** Each enabled action has an intentional recipient and observable result; repeated operating actions do not leave unexplained repeated customer messages.

**Retain:** Selected automation list and linked delivery results.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### stripe: Connect the operator-owned payment account

**Phase:** payments.

Record the operator's chosen Stripe connection and use its supported test or operator-approved pilot flow; follow the hosted payment link through to the invoice result.

**Completed outcome:** Payment belongs to the selected business account and reconciles to one invoice; the desk retains reference/status/amount rather than card details.

**Retain:** Payment-account custody, invoice reference and reconciliation.

**Published basis:** [Pricing and setup](https://ventcrm.com/pricing).

### payment_states: Reconcile a corrected or incomplete payment state

**Phase:** payments.

Select a relevant failed, reversed or manually recorded payment path with the operator. Preserve the original event and the correcting event together.

**Completed outcome:** The resulting amount due and customer document match the selected payment history without discarding the original invoice.

**Retain:** Original payment history, correction and resulting customer document.

**Published basis:** [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### self_export: Receive each promised self-serve export

**Phase:** portability.

From the selected instance, download the available self-serve entity exports and inventory their columns, counts, timestamps and source view.

**Completed outcome:** The recipient opens the actual files and reconciles the selected pilot records to the operating workspace.

**Retain:** Export manifest, original files and receiving notes.

**Published basis:** [Cancellation and data export](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation).

### complete_export: Account for the full handover scope

**Phase:** portability.

Build a coverage matrix for every agreed entity, relationship, document and attachment. Mark whether each comes from self-service, a supplied export or another agreed path.

**Completed outcome:** Every item in the selected handover has a real received output or a specifically assigned missing-output action; a smaller CSV set is not labeled complete.

**Retain:** Coverage matrix, missing-output ownership and received handover.

**Published basis:** [Cancellation and data export](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation).

### reopen: Reopen the same records from the delivered files

**Phase:** portability.

Open the handover in a clean receiving workspace using the agreed destination, retaining original identifiers. Follow one customer's linked job, invoice, payment and communication through the received records.

**Completed outcome:** The recipient can use the delivered information without the originating browser session, and exceptions remain connected to their source.

**Retain:** Receiving result, relationships and unresolved items.

**Published basis:** [Cancellation and data export](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation).

### exit_plan: Document the operator's selected exit treatment

**Phase:** handover.

Record the effective cancellation date, final billing, export destination and any agreed retention. Keep active-system removal and backup expiry as separate fields; preserve unset values when not established.

**Completed outcome:** The plan states what will happen and who owns it using the selected agreement and actual provider information.

**Retain:** Exit plan, agreement reference and backup-retention source.

**Published basis:** [Cancellation and data export](https://ventcrm.com/cancellation); [Terms of service](https://ventcrm.com/terms).

### walkthrough: Let the operator complete the working course

**Phase:** handover.

Have the operator use the saved pilot on the phone: find today's job, review access notes, continue the visit, invoice, receive a reply and locate its export. Open the same record on desktop.

**Completed outcome:** The operator can resume the saved work through the normal entry points and identify how to obtain support.

**Retain:** Completed course, usable guide and assigned follow-up work.

**Published basis:** [Pricing and setup](https://ventcrm.com/pricing); [Product tour and operator FAQ](https://ventcrm.com/).

### publication: Review the actual operator package

**Phase:** handover.

Review the outgoing guide, selected terms, screenshots and downloadable files as the operator will receive them. Keep private customer data out of the public sandbox and internal delivery destinations out of customer instructions.

**Completed outcome:** The delivered package is readable, current and opens from its actual recipient-facing surface; the public/demo policy scope is not silently applied to the private instance.

**Retain:** Final package edition, image review and recipient-ready handover.

**Published basis:** [Cookie policy](https://ventcrm.com/cookies); [Privacy policy](https://ventcrm.com/privacy).


## Questions to retain with the case

These are implementation/contract details not established by the retrieved public pages. Resolve them from the selected instance, current provider records and original account owner during the actual programme: recovery steps and any MFA requirement; import limits and attachment coverage; export schema and relationship keys; exact correction/refund workflow; backup-retention duration and restore procedure; service response commitments; domain and messaging-number transfer; provider-account custody; historical consent import; the operator's actual cutover date. Each question belongs in the existing case rather than a separate approval process.
