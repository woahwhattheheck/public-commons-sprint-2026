VentCRM operator profiles and commissioning desk

Open the complete package

Open the original sample.html or paid.html for the researched company profiles. The corresponding CSV and JSON preserve the original published contacts, observations, sources and tailored conversation openings.

Open sample-commissioning.html or paid-commissioning.html to work with the selected operators. Each is a complete local application. It contains its own source and needs no installation, login or network connection for editing. Source links open the original public websites separately.

The sample includes one operator. The paid edition includes its five original operators. The two researched replacement candidates remain in the internal source programme. An operator’s actual fleet, current system, data access, budget and interest are established during the conversation; the research profiles describe publicly observed workflows.

Enter actual programme details

Use Operators to read the original profile and proposed comparison. Enter your name at the top before recording changes.

In Setup, enter the agreed service scope, original system, operating timezone, cutover plan and business choices. Unknown values remain blank. Record the selected commercial agreement separately from the current published reference. As retrieved on October 13, 2026, VentCRM advertises USD750 setup and USD129 monthly, with messaging and card processing separate. The selected operator agreement determines the programme.

In Records, retain the actual original-file register, field and relationship mappings, received export list, account custody and assigned follow-up work. Store nonsecret references and received-file locations. Keep passwords, API keys, payment card data and unrelated customer information in their established systems. Rows can be edited or removed; the complete working history preserves their earlier values.

Source files should record their actual name, entity, count and SHA-256 when available. Mappings identify how each source field is mapped, retained or excluded. Export rows distinguish self-serve output from the wider agreed handover. Give unresolved work an owner and concrete output. Mark it done only after that output exists.

Complete one continuous operating course

The Acceptance section contains 24 source-linked operating steps. They cover the selected scope and terms; source receiving, mapping, reconciliation and cutover; owner and technician access; booking, messages and payments; and the complete export and handover. Use one authorized pilot from intake through its final received records. Each step includes its intended outcome and the output to retain.

Record the actual observed result and the person doing the work. Choose Not applicable only with the agreed scope reason. A passed source, mapping or custody step needs its corresponding working register. Self-serve and full-handover export steps require their respective received output routes.

The current public policy pages show published draft notices dated August 13, 2026. Keep those source versions distinct from the agreement selected for the private operator instance. Self-serve CSV is described for clients, invoices, leads and Stripe payouts; the wider cancellation handover names additional records and relationships. Record actual received coverage rather than assuming all entities are in an ordinary CSV.

Save, reopen and hand over

Working changes are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Use Save working JSON before closing or moving machines. The downloaded file contains the full source selection, all entered setup and registers, every check and decision, and the complete operation history.

Open working JSON receives that complete document, verifies its checksum and replays its history before replacing the local copy. A selected sample or paid desk accepts only the same selected research source. The empty general desk can open either complete programme.

The Handover section shows unfinished setup, mismatched migration counts and open work. Once the programme is complete, record the actual acceptance decision. Changing setup or registers resets its checks for review; editing a check reopens acceptance. Earlier values remain in the saved history. A second browser tab cannot silently overwrite a newer local copy.

Download the complete check register CSV and handover HTML for the receiving operator. The HTML includes source research, entered setup, every working register, results and the acceptance decision. The working JSON is the editable source.

Initial state and receiving

The supplied working JSON starts with blank operator-entered values and unreviewed checks. Open it in its accompanying desk to begin. Keep a downloaded original before working. Exercise your first edits in a separate copy if you are learning the interface.

Before handing over, open the exact selected HTML and all its accompanying CSV, JSON and guide files. Use both a desktop and a narrow phone view; save, reopen, edit and download the real outputs. Review the received records and source links together with the operator.
