# Application integration: the useful data-service installation

Use the following two paragraphs in the existing customer/deployment narrative. The complete [commissioning design](README.md) supplies original source and receiving commands for implementers.

**Useful customer operation.** Our data-service deployment starts with the buyer's actual task. A company-data consumer selects the company, reported concept, unit and filing/period window, retaining the original observations and accession history for its analysis. An OData consumer selects a typed entity collection and either a complete captured edition or a live reviewed query. Its original keys, exact numeric values, page continuation and source-access scope reach the receiving application together. The merchant chooses its resource and price; the buyer controls its actual request, wallet and persistent spending limit. The delivered outcome is usable data in that consumer and an operator able to change the source or terms and support the installation.

**Defined customer-host work.** The maintained SEC and OData products already provide these source and receiving contracts, with the original Applications, seller and buyer workflows. The funded integration configures the selected independent merchants' source access, deployment and downstream consumer, completes source/terms change and handover, and operates the same useful task through the selected facilitators and production recovery. The original two-merchant/two-buyer cohort, six cost packages, 400 hours/$38,000 and 150-day schedule measure those customer-host outcomes. For a paginated resource, we retain the complete query's useful result beside its individual approved page intents and payment records; actual repeat use and support effort guide the next implementation.
