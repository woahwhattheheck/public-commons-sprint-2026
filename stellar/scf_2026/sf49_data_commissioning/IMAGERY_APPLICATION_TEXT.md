# Application integration: a useful imagery service

Use this customer example alongside the existing SEC/OData and recurring-analysis design in the canonical application. The [complete imagery commissioning guide](IMAGERY.md) carries the same operation through source access, approved files, original merchant/buyer, GIS receiving and maintenance.

**Useful customer operation.** An imagery buyer selects the actual area, acquisition window, collection and image assets needed by its GIS task. The merchant's retained source, reviewed access policy, collection rights and exact selected files reach the buyer together. Its original purchase keeps the complete geographic result beside the individual approved dataset-page and file-transfer requests. The receiving team can open the same imagery, source metadata and rights in its own application and reopen the complete handover on another host. A later source or policy edition preserves the buyer's earlier acquired output.

**Defined customer-host work.** The maintained STAC, eoAPI and Prescient products already supply source capture, exact file handling, policy review, merchant preparation and the corresponding receiving contracts. The funded installation selects the independent merchant's real source and identity, permitted data, useful buyer selection, public terms, facilitator and receiving GIS application. It then completes the actual source/terms change, interrupted-operation recovery, production supervision and second-operator handover. This is prospective customer integration within the existing two-merchant/two-buyer cohort, six packages, 400 hours/$38,000 and 150-day award-relative plan.
