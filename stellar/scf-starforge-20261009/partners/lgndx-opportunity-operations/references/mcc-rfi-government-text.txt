L55: 
L56: The Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) seeks responses to the questions below. While desired, respondents are not required to provide answers to all questions. If you choose not to answer a question, please respond with “N/A” to the corresponding question number.
L57: Responses to this RFI should not exceed twenty (20) pages in total, excluding the cover page and table of contents. Respondents may allocate the available pages among the RFI questions as they deem appropriate and are encouraged to provide sufficient detail to fully address the questions and provide information that will assist the Government in conducting meaningful market research and refining the requirement and acquisition strategy.
L58: Responses should be clear, concise, and directly relevant to the information requested. Respondents are discouraged from including generic corporate marketing materials, promotional literature, or other information that does not directly address the Government's requirements or RFI questions.
L59: Respondents may also provide relevant recommendations, alternative approaches, lessons learned, or other information not specifically requested in this RFI if they believe such information would assist the Government in defining the requirement or acquisition strategy.
