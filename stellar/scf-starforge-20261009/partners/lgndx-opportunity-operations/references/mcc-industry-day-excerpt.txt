L54: Notice of Intent to Host a Virtual Industry Day
L55: The Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) intends to release a Request for Information (RFI) to support a provision requirement for all services required to provide applications engineering support, operate and maintain MCC’s database in a secure manner, develop solutions, and provide Tier 3 support across all enterprise applications to support the information technology requirements of Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO).
L56: The services to be acquired also include program management and administrative support necessary for the administration of this award. As background, the MCC network supports approximately 550 users, consisting of 250 Full-Time Employees (FTEs), and just under 300 non-employees. Approximately 95 percent (95%) of MCC staff are located at its facility in Washington D.C.
