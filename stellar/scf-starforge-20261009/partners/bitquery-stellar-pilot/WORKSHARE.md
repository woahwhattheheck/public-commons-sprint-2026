# Proposed Bitquery Stellar integration workshare

**USD 3,600 fixed fee; eight business days after the customer-specific scope and access are agreed.** This is a proposed implementation engagement. The public intake and merchant bridge already exist; the fee covers adapting, deploying and handing over a selected real customer product.

## Outcome

A Bitquery technical owner or introduced customer can turn an agreed Stellar delivery into an operable derived data product. It has exact asset/amount semantics, source and coverage identity, an operator query workspace, a priced native merchant route, buyer delivery and a repeatable refresh/recovery procedure. The engagement includes one approved source window/product, one customer staging deployment and its query plus immutable CSV delivery interfaces.

Three implemented derived views provide starting options: ledger activity, payment flows, and balance changes by date and asset. The customer chooses the useful product and fields; the remaining public components stay available without being charged as newly written software.

## Work and acceptance

| Work | Customer-specific delivery | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Refresh and transition | New source generation, preserved reader/file identity, existing deployment/recovery process, operator walkthrough and one defect remediation pass | Customer operator repeats the agreed update and recovery workflow and receives the maintained source/configuration handoff |

The proposal allocates approximately 24 hours of customer-specific engineering across eight business days. Days 1–2 establish the actual source contract and delivery. Days 3–4 adapt data handling and the chosen operator product. Days 5–6 integrate the existing merchant in customer staging. Days 7–8 complete the actual native flow, new-generation handoff and acceptance corrections.

Proposed payments are **USD 1,800 on source/product acceptance** and **USD 1,800 on merchant/operator acceptance**. Provider data fees, hosting and transaction expenses belong to the customer's approved service choices and are separate from this implementation fee. No provider purchase or subscription is created by this proposal.

## Technical basis

The [current Bitquery Stellar dataset](https://bitquery.io/datastore/datasets/stellar) supplies a concrete data product to integrate. The original implementation has already been exercised on its free samples through the existing native merchant stack. It includes a useful source discrepancy resolution: simple-payment enrichment can disagree with its directly joined operation amount, and the intake keeps the direct operation amount and full asset identity with an explicit resolution count.

The commercial discussion should select an approved customer use and deployment. Relevant uses include a payment operations report, an issued-asset activity view or a ledger activity service. The current implementation supports derived outputs; it does not require buying a full archive merely to demonstrate the workflow, and it does not promise a resale right to provider raw data.

## Inputs and delivery definition

The provider/customer designates the source and technical owner, approved output use, staging host, public payment choices, and existing authorized testnet infrastructure. Existing credentials stay in the operator environment. Customer-specific IP, license terms, milestones and payment method are agreed for the actual engagement.

The selected source product and staging deployment define the estimate. A different bulk-history contract, new indexer/database, custom business dashboard, public-network launch or ongoing operations can be priced from its actual needs. The existing code is the foundation for paid integration, rather than a promise to re-create software already delivered.
