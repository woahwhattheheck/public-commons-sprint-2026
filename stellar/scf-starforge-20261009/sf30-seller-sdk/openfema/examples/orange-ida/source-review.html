Editable engineering-output structures

Scope reconciliation

A memo for each parent project linking its location units, approved repair items, current conditions, source conflicts and engineering recommendations.

Fields: Unit ID · PW and version · Damaged inventory item · Original source file and page · Issued quantity and basis · Current measured quantity and measurement reference · Difference and proposed treatment · Reviewer and decision date

Quantity and estimate schedule

A location schedule that can be rolled up once per FEMA parent and issued RFP.

Fields: Location and station or repair limit · Work item · Unit · Measured quantity · Measurement calculation or drawing reference · Rate source and date · Estimate amount · Restoration or improvement classification · Funding decision reference

Permit and coordination matrix

A project-specific record of the approvals and utility or transport interfaces that affect the proposed works.

Fields: Agency or asset owner · Required decision or permit · Applicability reasoning · Design input · Condition to carry into the technical documents · Responsible engineer · Status and supporting source

Design and construction bid package

Coordinated plans, details, specifications, quantities and cost estimate with each item traceable to the accepted scope.

Fields: Drawing or specification reference · Related scope item · Design author and reviewer · Revision · City review outcome · Technical addendum reference

Construction and closeout record

A record linking installed quantities and payments to the approved scope, including changes and completed-work support.

Fields: Work item · Daily or inspection record · Installed quantity · Material or submittal reference · Payment application · Change authorization · As-built and photograph references · Scope variance explanation
