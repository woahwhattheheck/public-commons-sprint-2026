    Reading this view

    Each original supplier dispatch reserves one call. Routes using the same account share that allowance. Cached responses and completed request replays keep their existing behavior.

    A supplier cooldown follows its original rate-limit response. Capacity and retry times describe the saved snapshot; your next request checks the account again before dispatch.

    The JSON and CSV downloads contain the exact displayed snapshot, including every configured account. The search field filters the table.
