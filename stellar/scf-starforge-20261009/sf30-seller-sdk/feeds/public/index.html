Merchant and buyer handoff

Export the selected dataset, then prepare its original seller release with your public payment terms. The merchant command retains the exact edition, initializes its native version store and stages the original release.

node dataset-cli.mjs feeds merchant --help

Use the original seller's address and displayed snapshot to review its current offer and prepare buyer acquisition.

node dataset-cli.mjs feeds buyer --help

The complete command sequence and source semantics are included in feeds/README.md.
