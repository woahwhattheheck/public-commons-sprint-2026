Evidence and payment boundaries

Every imported cart is matched against its SHA-256 fingerprint. These exports are buyer-side review notes; no merchant identity or payment success is authenticated. The PayPal sandbox checkout stays in the existing ClaimProof app and requires separate human approvals. Third-party AG Grid script is loaded from the pinned jsDelivr CDN; browser content security policy disallows network uploads and external connections.

Use ClaimProof Commerce to generate exports first. This board never checks the payment rail and therefore cannot declare a purchase captured, settled, or unpaid.
