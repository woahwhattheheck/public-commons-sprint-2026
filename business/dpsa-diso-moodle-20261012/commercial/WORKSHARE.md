## Production scope

### 1. Agree the course and target environment — days 1–3

We review the prime's approved learning outcomes, source references, brand assets, target audience and the exact Moodle 4.5 deployment. Together we establish a module-by-module map, the available native question types, the authoring route, permitted roles, review responsibilities and the complete deliverable inventory.

The prime designates the qualified security SME and instructional-design authority. They approve the content baseline and identify local policy or terminology changes. The prime also confirms that the native editable source route and the disclosed pre-existing material are acceptable for its intended customer contract.

**Acceptance:** the named reviewers, authorised environment, 13-module inventory, source/rights schedule and dated review checkpoints are recorded.

### 2. Adapt the teaching and assessment — days 4–12

We adapt all 26 units to the agreed voice and learning context, integrate the SME's supplied corrections, retain the source-to-claim links, and align the objectives, teaching, applied workbook and worked answers. We revise the complete 312-question set where needed and preserve the seven existing interaction formats and meaningful feedback.

Each unit retains a twelve-question graded assessment, a personal application task and separate guidance. The delivered assessment configuration uses a 75% pass threshold, unlimited attempts and immediate grading. Worked answers and reflection guidance become available after the learner completes the personal workbook activity.

**Acceptance:** all 26 units are present and editable; their objectives, examples, workbooks, answers and assessments agree; the named content reviewer has a complete source edition to review.

### 3. Produce the complete media set — days 13–17

We produce 13 narrated module presentations from the editable scripts and 52 scenes, using the approved visual treatment and voice. The included production envelope is up to forty minutes of finished narration across the course. Deliverables include MP4 video, MP3 audio, VTT/SRT captions, transcripts, scripts, scene text and editable graphics.

The source already contains approximately thirty minutes of planned scene time; that is an editing estimate, not a measured runtime. The final delivery records actual duration and file sizes. Text and audio alternatives accompany the videos. We use a simple presentation treatment suitable for small screens and restrained bandwidth.

**Acceptance:** all 13 media sets play in the agreed environment; captions and transcripts follow the actual narration; the prime receives the original editable script and visual sources with each final output.

### 4. Assemble and configure native Moodle — days 18–22

We produce the complete course on one authorised Moodle 4.5 staging site. We configure the course structure, navigation, enrolment roles supplied by the prime, grade/completion rules, workbook-to-guidance sequence and all assessment types. The package contains the editable learning material and the original Moodle course backup rather than relying on access to a supplier's private authoring account.

The standard route is the native Moodle course and editable JSON/HTML/media source already supplied. If the prime requires Storyline/Rise project files or a different runtime format, the authoring decision and its production scope must be agreed before accepting this quote.

**Acceptance:** all modules and media are installed; the reviewer can navigate the course as learner and editor, inspect the assessment settings, and identify the source edition behind the staged course.

### 5. Complete two consolidated review cycles — days 23–30

We run two review cycles across the complete course, with one consolidated response from the prime at each checkpoint. We correct agreed content, assessment, visual, accessibility, navigation and operating defects, retaining the affected unit/source version and the resulting course version.

The receiving process covers all 26 graded assessments, the seven interaction formats, an unsuccessful attempt followed by a successful retry, saved learner progress, the workbook/guidance sequence, captioned media, and ordinary desktop/mobile operation. We inspect the resulting grades and completion state in the original Moodle course and repeat the relevant checks after a correction.

**Acceptance:** the final reviewed edition resolves agreed scope defects and carries the named review record and actual receiving evidence.

### 6. Deliver the full source and staff handover — days 31–35

We deliver the complete editable course, all generated media and captions, the original Moodle backup, file/source manifest, configuration, source/license notices, review record and practical operating guide. We restore the course in one agreed clean receiving environment and conduct two remote handover sessions of up to ninety minutes each for the prime's course editor and operator.

The receiving operator edits a selected teaching item and assessment, republishes the course, reopens it, and follows the supplied backup/restore instructions. The final package contains the source and tools used to produce it.

**Acceptance:** the designated prime reviewer signs the complete deliverable inventory and receiving record, including the course/source edition, media and original backup. A demonstrated scope defect stays assigned for correction; additional scope is priced before implementation.
