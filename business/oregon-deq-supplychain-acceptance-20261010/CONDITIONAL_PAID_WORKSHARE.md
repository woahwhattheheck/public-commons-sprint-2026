This is an illustrative TJLabs quote, *not* Oregon's allocated budget or a customer agreement. Deliverables due within five business days **after** prime provides legally shareable input, confirmed schema mapping and written scope approval:

2. Per-product source-to-market graph completeness report with actual evidence-pointer ages, unknown-source gaps and unlinked-facility queue; original input hash recorded.
3. Environmental/social risk category coverage matrix across the discovered product paths, separating `risk_found`, `reviewed_clear`, and absent/unknown evidence. No inferred clean bill of health.
4. Focused operator acceptance session with 5-product results, corrected re-run, small handoff guide and explicit follow-on price/risks. Prime retains proposal, compliance assurances, buyer communications and legally authorized supplier datasets.
