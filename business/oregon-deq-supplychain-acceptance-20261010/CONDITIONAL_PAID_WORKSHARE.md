# Proposed product-trace QA workshare

## One current offer

**$2,400 fixed fee for up to ten agreed building-material product traces from one authorized export, delivered in five business days from an agreed start date.** The start follows representative-sample review, agreed record/link limits and field mapping, permitted data access, and confirmed delivery availability.

This is the current proposal for the existing Oregon DEQ / Sourcemap conversation. It replaces the earlier five-product and three-product alternatives in this file. Historical alternatives remain in repository history; the current conversation uses this single scope and price.

The work serves a platform partner preparing or delivering material-tracing work. The partner supplies its existing data and platform knowledge; TokenJunkieLabs supplies the export-review workflow and the agreed QA delivery. The current proposal is export-based, with API/ERP integration, GIS validation, supplier discovery, platform implementation and ongoing support available only through separate scope.

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | What the partner receives |
| --- | --- |
| Agreed source and field mapping | A retained copy of the authorized export, its file identities, the selected product IDs, the mapping decisions and agreed review settings. Original IDs and values remain traceable to their source rows. |
| Reproducible product-trace review | The original graph/evidence audit together with the operator's complete row disposition, branch and reference findings, and preserved corrections. |
| Findings register | Missing or ambiguous lineage, inconsistent references, unsupported risk annotations, unresolved source records, and each reviewer's recorded disposition. |
| Corrected rerun | One correction/rerun for agreed-checklist nonconformities identified during the acceptance period. The original and corrected inputs and results remain available for comparison. |
| Delivery package | The reviewed export, record-level reconciliation, findings package, agreed CSV/XLSX outputs, rerun instructions and a one-page acceptance report. |

The original audit reports one selected source-to-market path per product. The operator's row and branch reconciliation supplies the wider review context. The acceptance report identifies both views so a documented selected path cannot conceal unresolved records elsewhere in the supplied trace.

Missing supplier records remain visible findings for the designated reviewer. A partner can accept a correctly performed QA delivery that identifies data gaps; the contractual checklist should assess the quality and completeness of the agreed work.

## Inputs and start of delivery

The partner provides:

1. Up to ten named product/article IDs and the relevant finished-product or destination nodes.
2. The authorized source export and its data dictionary, including product/component, facility, relationship and evidence records available for that sample.
3. A contact who can resolve field meanings, source classifications and disputed mappings.
4. The agreed record/link limits, source snapshot, freshness setting and review checklist.
5. Any required subcontract or data-handling approvals and the permitted location and recipients for retained files.

The five-business-day delivery window begins on the agreed start date after these inputs are reviewed. A different source system, additional traces, new integration or materially larger source graph is handled through an agreed scope change.

Use [SOURCE_MAPPING.md](SOURCE_MAPPING.md) to prepare the export. It distinguishes published vendor data roles from the local audit contract and explains the mapping decisions the partner needs to review.

## Delivery plan

| Stage | Work and handoff |
| --- | --- |
| Start / day 1 | Confirm the selected source files and product IDs, resolve the field mapping, agree the checklist and freeze the original input version. |
| Days 2–4 | Run the source reconciliation and original graph audit, investigate supplied-record inconsistencies, retain the reviewer decisions, and prepare the corrected export and findings package. |
| Day 5 | Deliver the complete package, rerun instructions and one-page acceptance report to the designated partner reviewer. |
| Acceptance period | The partner has three business days to review against the agreed checklist. One correction/rerun for checklist nonconformities is included. |

The actual start date and review recipient belong in the signed statement of work.

## Acceptance checklist

The signed checklist should identify the exact sample, mappings, review settings and output files. Acceptance requires:

- Every supplied source row in scope has a reproducible disposition: mapped to its target record, retained as an explicit review item, or excluded with a recorded reason.
- Selected products and their supplied nodes, links and evidence references can be traced back to the original files and rows.
- Missing or conflicting identifiers, references, lineage and supporting evidence appear in the findings package.
- The original source-to-market audit can be reproduced with the same input version and settings.
- Corrected values and reviewer decisions retain their earlier versions and source association.
- The agreed output files, rerun instructions and acceptance report are complete and reproducible.
- The designated partner reviewer gives written sign-off against the agreed checklist.

The package describes the supplied evidence and its review state. Material-origin substantiation, independent supplier verification and resolution of absent supplier data require the corresponding source work and responsible experts.

## Fee and payment terms

The proposed fee is **$2,400** for the scope above. The signed statement of work will confirm payment terms and any required subcontract or data-handling approvals. Payment is tied to acceptance of this QA workshare independently of the procurement award.

Confirm the currency, tax treatment and payment due date in the signed statement of work alongside the agreed source, checklist and delivery schedule.

## Partner responsibilities

The partner assesses its own bidding eligibility and retains responsibility for platform licensing, permitted source data, certification and insurance evidence, service commitments, prime pricing, agency correspondence and submission. TokenJunkieLabs delivers the agreed export-based QA package and application handoff.

The proposed workshare is separate from API/ERP integration, GIS validation, supplier discovery, the commercial tracing platform and ongoing operations. Additional work can be described and priced after the partner identifies the actual system, inputs and delivery need.

## Why the partner route fits

Sourcemap's current [partner program](https://www.sourcemap.com/partner) includes systems integration and consulting. Its [integration page](https://www.sourcemap.com/technology/erp-integration) describes bulk upload and a REST interface for enterprise data. The proposed workshare uses a partner-authorized export and an agreed mapping, which allows the existing platform and delivery team to remain the operational owner.

The partner's designated reviewer, agreed source snapshot and written checklist provide the handoff between the application, the delivered QA work and acceptance.
