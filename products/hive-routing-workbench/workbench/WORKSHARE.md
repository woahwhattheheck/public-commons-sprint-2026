# Hive application implementation workshare

## Proposed engagement

Implement and hand over one complete GraphQL application on the original Hive Router, including the complete client programme, reviewed migration changes, operational cutover/rollback and a fresh receiving installation.

**Proposed fixed price: USD 6,400.**  
**Planned delivery: 10 business days after kickoff and agreed application access.**  
**Payment schedule: USD 3,200 at kickoff and USD 3,200 at accepted handover.**

This offer is prepared for implementation-partner work through The Guild's publicly stated partner route. The application and schedule below are the proposed customer delivery.

## Customer scope

One customer application, up to three selected customer subgraphs, twenty agreed client operations, one selected existing host and a fresh receiving installation on that host or the agreed receiving host.

The public engineering reference retains all four original upstream fixture subgraphs: accounts, inventory, products and reviews. The fixture demonstrates the complete operator lifecycle independently of the customer's application inventory.

The engagement includes the complete original application edition and one reviewed changed edition, two engineering review sessions, a 60-minute handover, and ten business days of correction for defects in the agreed delivered implementation.

## Acceptance

The original and changed application editions carry all selected files. Each agreed operation has its complete native HTTP/GraphQL result and a reviewed reason. The initial, changed and rollback programmes each execute the complete agreed operation set.

Authentication, request/response headers, persisted operations and existing telemetry are reviewed against the selected original configuration. Expected authorization, validation or application errors are captured and reviewed alongside successful data.

Cutover selects the reviewed current native process. Rollback restores the retained earlier application through a new complete programme from its actual restarted process.

The complete delivered ZIP opens on the receiving installation with the saved source, original edition files, responses, reviews and history. That installation starts its original router and runs the agreed complete client programme.

## Customer inputs

The customer supplies the selected application source, existing subgraph endpoints and access, current router/gateway configuration, the actual twenty-operation client programme and the selected host access. The delivery design records the intended authentication and authorization outcomes and the planned deployment window.

Additional applications, subgraphs, client programmes, new third-party services or infrastructure migrations can be priced as a further defined workshare after the current application inventory is agreed.

## Handover

The customer receives the editable delivered operator source, original source provenance, complete saved application editions, operating and receiving guides, native outcome history, reviewed cutover/rollback decisions and the agreed implementation work.

The technical handover follows the delivered source and complete workspace. Commercial acceptance follows the agreed customer application scope and complete working receiving edition.
