# Arrangr company expansion pilot — campaign and response kit

Prepared for the existing Arrangr team. These are editable materials for one reviewed pilot. The operating desk records actual exposure, contact and later source events; it does not dispatch these messages.

## 1. In-product invitation

**Placement:** a relevant existing scheduling or account surface for an eligible, verified member in the selected company cohort.

**Heading:** Bring your scheduling into one team

**Body:** Keep your own scheduling setup while giving your team one place to manage members and billing. Choose the right membership for each colleague and try the workflow with one real meeting.

**Primary action:** Review a team setup

**Secondary action:** Keep my current setup

**Destination:** the existing organization setup route, https://arrangr.com/app/sign_up?acct_type=org , or the corresponding existing authenticated team surface selected by the product owner.

**Event mapping:** record the actual visible invitation as offer_exposed with the original user ID and pilot ID. A click or opened form is a distinct interaction; it does not establish team creation, accepted membership or billing. Keep the existing account state when the member declines.

## 5. Practical reply guidance

### Member plans and billing

Use the current team offer and the customer's actual period/invoice when explaining charges. Confirm each member's chosen plan and the billing owner. Keep a product activity count separate from chargeable usage; the public pages do not define the full billing-event, proration or invoice-cutoff rule.

Sources: https://get.arrangr.com/teams_smbs/ and https://get.arrangr.com/pricing-and-plans/

### Admin ownership and rollout

Confirm the person who will own the existing organization account. Work through the existing invite/member setup with that owner. Retain the original organization and member identifiers, then verify actual accepted membership and a confirmed scheduling event. Use the product's current control names and permissions during installation.

The original product setup and dashboard guides are useful references:
- https://arrangr.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/47001248634-set-up-your-account
- https://arrangr.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/47001248636-your-arrangr-dashboard
- https://arrangr.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/47001248641-booking-pages-overview

### Calendar access

Arrangr's current privacy policy describes calendar tokens used for reading/adding events and settings controlling displayed availability. It says other users see busy times without event details. Calendar connections can be unlinked or revoked through the provider.

Source: https://get.arrangr.com/privacy-policy/

### AI data handling

The privacy policy describes Ari sending a prompt and relevant scheduling context to an external model provider. It identifies Google's paid Gemini API as the current primary provider and describes separate optional internal prompt review. Use that specific policy when answering questions about external processing.

Source: https://get.arrangr.com/privacy-policy/

### Hosting and security review

The privacy policy identifies Google Cloud servers in the United States and encrypted web pages. Provide the policy and route a requested security questionnaire, contractual requirement or certification statement to the product owner for the current official response.

Source: https://get.arrangr.com/privacy-policy/

## 6. Measurement and operator handoff

Keep the original pilot ID, fixed user cohort and baseline. The existing engine evaluates these distinct observations:

| Stage | Required source |
| --- | --- |
| Exposure | Actual offer_exposed event with original user and pilot |
| Team creation | Actual team_created event bound to pilot and organization |
| Accepted membership | team_invite_accepted with the same pilot and organization |
| Confirmed use | Original cohort member's confirmed scheduling event after acceptance |
| Activated team | At least two such accepted-and-confirmed original members in one pilot-created organization |
| Subscription outcome | Original dated subscription snapshots |
| Collected outcome | Actual signed payment/refund receipts |

Log the actual contact using one of the maintained outcomes: sent, no_reply, positive_reply, meeting_requested, human_review, unsubscribed, do_not_contact, hard_bounce or other. Preserve the original timestamp and reference. Company grouping, account eligibility and contact cooldowns are recalculated from the retained record.

After the first meaningful operating cycle, save the full workspace. Reopen that exact edition before continuing. Review observed changes alongside the initial baseline, and keep any causal claim separate from the descriptive before/after result.
