# Arrangr company expansion pilot — proposed workshare

## Outcome

Give the existing Arrangr team one repeatable operating workflow for identifying promising company groups among current users, selecting an appropriate champion, and reviewing a team pilot against its original starting cohort.

The prepared Expansion Desk already supplies the company queue, original-source imports, saved pilots, contact and suppression history, and subscription/payment outcome review. The installed work connects that operating record to the actual product data and makes it usable by the person responsible for the pilot.

## Installed scope

One existing Arrangr operating environment, one agreed data-export path, and one company-expansion pilot:

1. Map the original user, organization and product-event records to the desk's maintained data contract. Retain stable identifiers, timestamps and source references.
2. Map subscription observations and payment/refund receipts independently. Preserve each currency and keep active-member analysis separate from the actual billing definition.
3. Review business-domain grouping against actual organization identity. Record explicit overrides for ambiguous domains and retain contact permissions and suppression.
4. Configure the active window, dormant-member window, contact cooldown and eligibility threshold with the operator.
5. Select one eligible company, record its owner and freeze the baseline/member cohort. Prepare the actual sequence for the existing organization/admin and member setup workflow.
6. Receive subsequent observed invitation, product use, subscription and payment records; retain the pilot's original cohort and compare current outcomes.
7. Hand over the complete source, standalone application, source mappings, original working record and an operating guide. Reopen the saved edition with the operator so the next review continues from the same history.

The existing scheduler, team account workflow, member plans and billing system continue to own their current behavior. This scope adds the repeatable expansion operating record around those systems. External messages are handled through the existing relationship owner; the desk records what happened and retains the source.

## Completion

The handover includes a complete customer working record containing actual mapped source exports, a reviewed company queue, one saved pilot, and a later observation edition. The operator can open the company, inspect its original members and contact history, review the fixed baseline and subsequent outcomes, and save/reopen the full record with the same identifiers and journal.

Billing observations are reported as observed. They are not replaced by a manually selected conversion status. The pilot review keeps exposure, confirmed product activity, subscription state and collected net amounts separate.

## Commercial proposal

Proposed fixed price: US$2,400 for this one installed pilot milestone, with US$1,200 at start and US$1,200 at handover. Proposed schedule: ten business days after the agreed original exports, event meanings and operating access are available, followed by fourteen calendar days of corrections to the delivered work.

This is a proposed initial installed pilot within the staged engagement described in the hiring post. The post says the first two milestones would be contracted first; the owner can place this scope within that agreed sequence.

## Prepared example and handover

The attached standalone desk includes a clearly labelled demonstration. It can also open complete original workspaces and import CSV/JSON tables with explicit mapping. Its source editions, pilot baselines and operation journal are retained in the complete download.

The sample does not require customer account access. The installation uses the actual original product records and the owner's existing contact process.

## Product basis

Arrangr's current team offer already provides central billing, admin account management, mixed Basic/Pro membership and active-member pricing. The proposed work uses these existing capabilities as the destination of a company pilot.
