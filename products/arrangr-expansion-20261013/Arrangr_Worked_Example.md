# A company pilot that keeps its original cohort

Open the attached Arrangr Expansion Desk, choose **Load demonstration**, then **Completed pilot**. This is an illustrative operating example built with fictional organizations and source events. The same desk opens a complete original customer workspace.

## Starting decision

The original queue contains 12 users in seven company groups. Two groups qualify for expansion, one is dormant, one is already an observed team, two are separate personal-email users, and one is suppressed.

Green Lane starts as two eligible users on the same exact business email domain. Both have recent scheduling activity. Its existing personal subscription observation is USD 600 minor units, or $6 monthly. The pilot freezes those two original user IDs and that starting recurring amount.

Choose **Original company queue** to inspect that earlier edition. Company detail shows the source members, reasons, proposed champion, permissions and original activity. It lets an operator prepare a pilot with an owner and reason.

## What changes in the completed edition

The retained later source shows a new Green Lane organization, and the two same users now belong to it. The old domain-based pilot keeps its original identity and cohort.

The source then records:

- The offer was exposed to both original members.
- The team was created with the original pilot and organization identifiers.
- Both members accepted that organization's invitation.
- Each member subsequently had confirmed scheduling activity in the same organization.
- The personal subscription was cancelled and an active team subscription was observed.
- An $18 payment and a $2 refund were recorded.

The completed view shows two exposed members, two accepted invitations, two confirmed active members and an observed activated team. This requires the invitation and confirmed-activity sequence; general activity alone does not set the activated outcome.

These values come from the example's subscription snapshots and receipts. They are not calculated from published per-seat rates. The recurring change is a before/after observation, and organization-level billing may include seats outside an original cohort.

## Operate the same record

Open **Pilot outcomes** to inspect the saved baseline and supporting event/payment IDs. Use **Data and sources** to inspect the actual retained import editions. Use **Operation history** to inspect the original prepare/import operations.

Save the complete workspace and reopen it. The original pilot, source rows, member IDs, subscription observations and payment/refund records remain in the same edition. Pause or close with a reason when an operator stops the pilot.

The installed workshare maps the actual product exports into this workflow and operates one real company pilot through the owner's existing team setup and contact process.
