Snowpack's published guidance on data contracts and slim dbt CI supports a concrete handover need: keep a client's original source, original execution results and agreed acceptance attached to a delivery that the client can operate after the project. ## Proposed paid delivery

TJLabs can take one agreed existing client dbt/Dagster reporting domain through implementation and operating handover under Snowpack's review. The first scoped project would include:

- the agreed original input/project intake and one existing warehouse/runtime binding
- implementation or repair of the agreed SQL/YAML models and their existing/native acceptance checks
- original Dagster/dbt execution and recovery against the agreed examples
- client-facing source/lineage/result review and a usable release/operator handover
- source, installed configuration references, run history and receiving/reopen instructions

The proposed first project is US$6,000, with ten business days after both parties agree the original inputs, access and acceptance examples;50% to begin and50% on acceptance. Snowpack keeps its client relationship and reviews the work. The client's platform/cloud usage stays on the client's account. These are proposed terms, not a contract, invoice, payment or promised outcome.

This workspace is a useful implementation asset for that delivery. It does not replace the client's dbt or Dagster project with a new orchestration framework. It retains the actual native source, results and operating handover.

The implementation scope and price above are a proposal for discussion. A customer project begins after its inputs, operating environment, acceptance examples and commercial terms are agreed.
