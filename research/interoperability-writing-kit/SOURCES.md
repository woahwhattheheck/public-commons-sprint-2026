## Source authority

The WR identifiers belong to this sample. They govern the proposed reporting convention. They do not add requirements to OpenTDF, an IETF RFC, or a standards body's publication.

The OpenTDF pages are current public documentation as retrieved. A real execution records its exact installed source/API/schema and selected standard/profile edition; the report does not infer them from a moving website. Examples deliberately avoid fabricated endpoint paths, response enums, status codes, propagation intervals or implementation results.

OpenTDF documentation states CC BY 4.0 licensing in its footer. The kit links and paraphrases a small amount of its public architecture and interface explanation; all added prose and working templates are TJLabs-authored. IETF documents are cited by their original RFC Editor URLs.

## Review focus

The important editorial decisions are explicit: transport failure differs from policy denial; authentication, authorization and enforcement have separate observations; retries preserve their earlier run; a configuration-change result states time and client state; a cross-implementation comparison preserves each original request; and the conclusion carries the measured scope. These are authored reporting choices explained in SAMPLE.md.
