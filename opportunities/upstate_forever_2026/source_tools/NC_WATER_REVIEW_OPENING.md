## What completes and what is saved

The helper writes request.json once with the actual project identity, Workspace, selected geography, planning companion identities and NC manifest hash. An original operation lock protects that output. Repeating the same request resumes the maintained producer's immutable edition; a different request cannot silently occupy that output.

Complete original hashes, compound classes, per-record dates and source metadata remain unchanged. Both the source's printed schedule edition and the service capture date stay visible.
