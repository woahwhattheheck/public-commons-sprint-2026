## Match and interpretation

The join uses the exact station identifier, constituent parameter code and COMID. It does not choose by station name or assume a composite station is the same as its flow gage. The selected row retains its original merged-station text, flow-station identifier, publisher drainage areas, sampling counts, censor counts and both source periods. Unmatched, ambiguous or unusable records remain explicit rows; none of the original eleven targets disappears.

The national metadata selects `beale` using `rload_a_XXXXX` with `serload_a_XXXXX`, or `flux5pkd` using `LOAD_A_XXXXX` with `SLOAD_A_XXXXX`. The companion checks those original fields against the existing importer's values. National load and standard-error values are kg/year. For a regional target in metric tonnes/year, both are divided by 1000 once. Nitrogen 00600, phosphorus 00665, TSS 00530 and SSC 80154 remain separate constituents.

Regional service masses are preserved. The companion reports the national-minus-regional difference in both kg/year and the original regional unit instead of assuming equality or replacing the existing target. Published national standard errors stay attached to the corresponding national estimate. They are not confidence bounds for a restoration response, a model prediction interval, a detection limit, or an error estimate transferred automatically to a different regional edition.

Water-quality sampling periods and flow periods are separate original fields. The national importer preserves the included final flow day and supplies the following day as an exclusive end. The regional output's source-global 1999–2014 horizon and 2012 reference year remain attached to every original regional target. The whole-basin area is the retained NLDI polygon area; publisher gage drainage areas remain source context. All original basin overlap/containment relations remain in the joined output.
