Methods, scope and exact-source retention

One analytical series has one station, source project, characteristic, unit, sample fraction, chemical specification, analytical method, method context and value type. Different series are never pooled. Agreeing multiple result IDs from one activity receive a single measurement weight; every original ID remains in the inspector and retained original CSV. Conflicting values receive no numerical summary.

All arithmetic uses exact rational endpoints. Quantified measurements remain points; below-limit measurements retain their original lower bound and open upper reporting limit; above-limit measurements retain their open lower bound and unbounded upper limit. An open parenthesis excludes an endpoint. No measurement is replaced by zero, half its limit or a fitted value.

Months containing an excluded or conflicting measurement are retained but omitted from the fully supported calendar-month match. Every originally excluded measurement remains unchanged in the inspector. Arithmetic pH summaries are summaries of reported pH values, not mean hydrogen-ion concentrations. Observed grab samples do not determine annual mass loads, regulatory compliance or causes of change.
