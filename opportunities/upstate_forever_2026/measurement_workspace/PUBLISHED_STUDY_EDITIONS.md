# Published Study source editions

The primary study.json belongs at the root of the complete study companion. A materialized companion may have one enclosing directory. Metadata called study.json inside a retained original source family remains an original document: it does not declare another primary study, and it is never removed or rewritten during root selection. Separate sibling study roots remain ambiguous and are rejected.

## Complete context creates a distinct edition

The installed SV-833, Three and Twenty 2026, and Three and Twenty historical builders retain registered context through measurement_workspace/study_context.py. The four-station river companion includes its complete native source manifest, every declared original/report/table/code companion, and family.json. All retained bytes enter the study companion.

PublishedStudyStore.import_source computes the study version from the sorted inventory of every retained path, byte count, and SHA256. Changing a context report, source, manifest, or inventory therefore produces a different study version. The source collection also changes: its native entries retain their original source IDs within that collection, and their metadata binds the exact study version. The nineteen original geographic/observation entries enter through the retained native manifest; additional source documents are registered separately.

A normal import updates only the study's current pointer. It keeps older version directories and source collections. Saved reviews retain the exact study/version, collection, manifest hash, and full source-inventory hash; reopening resolves and verifies that binding. Importing a review uses make_current=False and preserves an existing current selection. Whole-study export retains every source body and reopening derives the same original version.
