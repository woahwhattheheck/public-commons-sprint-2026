L517@P15: Model Development
L518@P15: Regression analysis was done using R by examining turbidity and other continuously measured data as
L519@P15: explanatory variables for estimating suspended-sediment concentration. A variety of models that predict SSC and
L520@P15: models that predict log10(SSC) were evaluated. The distribution of residuals was examined for normality and plots
L521@P15: of residuals (the difference between the measured and predicted values) as compared to predicted SSC were
L522@P15: examined for homoscedasticity (meaning that their departures from zero did not change substantially over the
L523@P15: range of predicted values). This comparison of several models led to the conclusion that the most appropriate and
L524@P15: reliable model would be one that estimated log10(SSC).
L525@P15: Turbidity and streamflow were selected as the best predictors of log10(SSC) based on residual plots, relatively high
L526@P15: adjusted coefficient of determination (adjusted R2), and relatively low model standard percentage error (MSPE).
L527@P15: Values for all of the aforementioned statistics and metrics were computed and are included below along with all
L528@P15: relevant sample data and more in-depth statistical information.

L532@P15: log10(𝑆𝑆𝑆) = 0.933 × log10(𝑇𝑇𝑇𝑇) + 0.0431 × log10(𝑄) + 0.262 ,
L533@P15: where
L534@P15: SSC = suspended-sediment concentration, in milligrams per liter (mg/L);
L535@P15: Q = streamflow in cubic feet per second (ft3/s); and,
L536@P15: TURB = Turbidity, YSI model 6136, in formazin nephelometric units (FNU).
L537@P15: The use of turbidity and streamflow as explanatory variables is appropriate physically and statistically. Turbidity
L538@P15: makes sense physically because suspended sediment is composed of particles that scatter light in water.
L539@P15: Suspended sediment correlates well with streamflow because high streamflow values tend to increase
L540@P15: concentrations of SSC. The relation between turbidity and SSC can vary given varying concentrations of organic
L541@P15: suspended particles that increase turbidity, but are not included in the SSC analysis.
L542@P15: The log-transformed model may be retransformed to the original units so that SSC can be calculated directly. The
L543@P15: retransformation introduces a bias in the calculated constituent. This bias may be corrected using Duan’s Bias
L544@P15: Correction Factor (BCF). For this model, the calculated BCF is 1.02. The retransformed model, accounting for BCF
L545@P15: is:
L546@P15: 𝑆𝑆 = 1.865 × 𝑇𝑇𝑇 0.933 × 𝑄0.0431.
