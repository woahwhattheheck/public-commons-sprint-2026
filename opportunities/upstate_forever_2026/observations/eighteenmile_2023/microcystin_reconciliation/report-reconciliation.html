Reporting limits and precision

The selected originals contain 17 below-limit records: four with 0.1 µg/L limits and thirteen with 0.016 µg/L limits. The source-informed interval calculation retains each actual limit. It is displayed separately from the report-caption substitution.

At SV-839, the original values yield a mean of 0.050433 µg/L (0.050 at three decimal places). Rounding individual quantified values to 0.001 before aggregation changes the mean to 0.050667 (0.051), but changes the displayed standard deviation to 0.027. This sensitivity explains how precision can affect the last digit; it does not establish the publisher's calculation.
