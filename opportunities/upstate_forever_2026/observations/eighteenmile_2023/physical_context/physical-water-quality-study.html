The recurring pattern across twelve dates

SV-376 was cooler on all 12 paired dates and had higher dissolved oxygen and turbidity on 11. E. coli was higher on nine. The phosphorus concentration was lower at SV-376 on ten dates, yet its mean paired difference is positive because of the June 12 observation.

Read each comparison with its clock. SV-376 was sampled 129–235 minutes before SV-268 on every paired date. Temperature and oxygen vary over a day, so site and sampling time are entangled in this cohort. These differences describe the observations; attributing them to an individual source or a reach process would require additional evidence.

The phosphorus difference on that date was +0.58 mg/L. Across all 12 dates, the mean difference is [0.00525, 0.01025) mg/L, while the median is [-0.042, -0.036] mg/L. As a single-observation sensitivity calculation, removing June 12 changes the mean for the other 11 dates to [-0.047, -0.041545) mg/L. The full 12-date result remains the primary result.

Turbidity follows the same influence pattern: June 12 contributes a +580 NTU difference. The full mean is 60.408 NTU and the median is 5 NTU; the other 11 dates average 13.173 NTU. This is a direct reason to examine the individual observations before interpreting the sampled-date mean.

The E. coli result at SV-376 is >9,678.4 MPN/100mL. Its blank result value and original upper quantitation limit define a right-unbounded interval. Consequently, the 12-date mean E. coli difference has no finite upper bound. Its median difference remains exactly 523.75 MPN/100mL because the censored June value falls beyond the middle two ordered differences.

Daily gauge flow describes watershed conditions on the observation date. It is not an instantaneous discharge measured at each station. The co-occurrence of high flow, turbidity, phosphorus, and bacteria on June 12 identifies a useful event for investigation; it does not identify a source or establish a rainfall mechanism.

Intervals preserve both kinds of laboratory limits

Below-quantitation results use [0, limit); above-quantitation results with a blank measured value and an upper quantitation limit use (limit, +∞). Thus 34 of 44 biochemical oxygen demand results remain below 2 mg/L, and the four upper-censored E. coli values keep their open upper tails. A numerical reported value is retained as the reported value. Mean and median bounds propagate through both the observation and difference calculations.

Paired difference = value at station B − value at station A
Reconstructed total nitrogen = same-activity Kjeldahl nitrogen + nitrate/nitrite

Both nitrogen components must use the retained unfiltered fraction and compatible original method identifiers. The biochemical oxygen demand measurements retain method 5210-B. BOD describes the oxygen microorganisms consume during the prescribed incubation measurement; it is a different quantity from the instantaneous dissolved-oxygen field reading. See USGS BOD methods background and the EPA WQX guide for detection-condition and quantitation metadata.
