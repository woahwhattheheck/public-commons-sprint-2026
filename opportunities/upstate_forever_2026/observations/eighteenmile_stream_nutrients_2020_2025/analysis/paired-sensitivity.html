Methods and original records

Each original pair has equal weight. Lower and upper difference endpoints are summed using exact 12-decimal integer arithmetic; means are rounded only after division. The exact endpoint sums, divisor, endpoint openness and included original data rows accompany all 148 selections. The result evaluates sensitivity to the available sampling dates, calendar years and reference-flow selection. Sampling uncertainty, laboratory error and causal attribution require separate analyses.
