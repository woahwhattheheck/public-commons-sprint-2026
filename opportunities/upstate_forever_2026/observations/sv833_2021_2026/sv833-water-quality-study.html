Association with censoring retained

For each pair of activities, the analysis determines whether their original concentration intervals require concordant or discordant ordering. Exact ties contribute zero. Unresolved orderings receive conservative −1 and +1 score bounds. Dividing by all unordered activity pairs gives the following Kendall tau-a score bounds.

Both lower bounds remain positive. These are conservative bounds on the pair-score statistic; independently assigning every unresolved pair may produce combinations that cannot all occur together. They are not confidence intervals.

Temperature and dissolved oxygen have a strong inverse relationship in these observations. This agrees with the general temperature dependence described in the USGS dissolved-oxygen guide. The correlation combines seasonal conditions and other local processes; it does not isolate a causal effect.

Method decisions

  1. Identity: ResultIdentifier joins the narrow result profile to the original quantitation-limit export. Original ActivityIdentifier joins sampling metadata. The WQP user guide describes both identifier relationships.
  2. Cross-profile reconciliation: Every narrow result matches the default profile on the original activity, site, characteristic, reporting condition, value, unit, fraction, method, result type, status, date, and clock fields.
  3. Repeated records: A repeated activity-characteristic group contributes once only when interval, unit, speciation, fraction, method, date, clock, and status agree. All underlying result IDs remain in the measurement index.
  4. Censoring: Below-limit concentrations use [0, L); above-limit concentrations use (L, +∞). Means average lower and upper endpoints separately. Medians sort endpoint bounds separately and preserve open endpoints. Null upper endpoints mean positive infinity for an existing measurement; an absent measurement has no original result IDs.
  5. Coverage: Annual and calendar-season summaries use actual observed activities. DJF, MAM, JJA, and SON refer to calendar months. No missing activity is filled with zero. Exact-pair associations report their exclusions, and interval-order analysis retains the original censored bounds.
  6. Hydrology: SV-833 is a distinct Three and Twenty Creek station. These results are concentration observations at that station; streamflow and load calculations require a verified corresponding hydrologic source.
