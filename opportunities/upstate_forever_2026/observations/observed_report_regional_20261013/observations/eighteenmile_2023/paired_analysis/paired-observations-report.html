Eighteenmile Creek: paired observations

Five sites, 15 shared chlorophyll dates and explicit microcystin reporting limits.

This comparison follows the original study cohort and preserves every supporting agency activity, recorded clock and source row. It contains 150 same-date chlorophyll contrasts and 128 microcystin contrasts linked to the selected chlorophyll observations.

Reading the observed pattern. SV-840, the Lake Hartwell station off the Eighteenmile arm, had lower chlorophyll than upper-lake-arm SV-838 on all 15 sampled dates. The mean paired difference (SV-840 minus SV-838) was -18.032 ug/L. Their recorded sampling times were 53–109 minutes apart. The linked microcystin pattern differed: SV-840 was higher on 7 dates, lower on 5 and unresolved by reporting limits on 1 date. These comparisons describe measured concentrations across sites and sampled dates.

4. Source and method

units: Original ug/L; source numbers and detection-limit units retained.

chlorophyll cohort: Existing original five-site CHLA project/calendar/displayed-clock reconciliation, unchanged.

chlorophyll pairing: Same original calendar date; each site has one selected result. Stations were sampled at different recorded times.

cross analyte pairing: Exact station, original date, displayed clock and timezone label; distinct agency project/activity identities retained. Same-date alternatives are displayed separately.

sample depth: The published study describes surface (0.3 m) microcystin sampling alongside chlorophyll (Cyanotoxins methods and Table 8). Individual selected CHLA activities encode 0.3 m; linked WQP microcystin activity depth and unit fields are blank. Preserve the report protocol and individual source fields together.

microcystin scope: All original SCDHEC-MICRO records at the five sites, plus a separately labeled exact-clock CHLA-linked subset.

censoring: A below-reporting-limit concentration is represented by [0, original reporting limit), assuming physical nonnegative concentration. Quantified observations are point values. Sample-mean bounds are interval arithmetic, not imputed estimates or confidence intervals.

paired microcystin: Only microcystin observations with exact recorded-clock CHLA links enter same-date spatial contrasts; both original interval endpoints propagate.

inference: Descriptive comparisons of retained 2023 sampled dates. No population inference, seasonal weighting, simultaneous-sample claim, hydrologic travel-time matching or concentration-to-load conversion.

timezones: Original EST labels remain unchanged; displayed-clock differences do not convert source timestamps to UTC.

printed report: Original printed sample counts and study statements remain in the published study source.

Original agency report: SCDES 2023 Lake Program final report. Complete original response collection: Upper Savannah primary sources.
