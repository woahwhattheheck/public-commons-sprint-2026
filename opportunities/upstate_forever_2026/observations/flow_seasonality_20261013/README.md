## Eighteenmile 2023 seasonal interpretation

At Eighteenmile Creek below Pendleton, the 2023 summer mean was 58.663 cfs, 33.85% above the median of its other complete summers. Autumn averaged 27.963 cfs, 46.56% below the corresponding autumn median. Summer carried 21.61% of complete 2023 volume compared with a 14.83% median share; autumn carried 10.19% compared with 18.31%. These temporal contrasts identify different hydrologic sampling contexts within the same annual period.

Each selected year is compared with other complete periods at the same gauge. The complete_numeric basis admits numeric values with their retained original flags. The fully_approved_complete basis requires A on every target and reference-period day; A:e remains both approved and estimated. Period-level reference years and annual-fraction reference years are recorded separately. For Eighteenmile 2023, approved summer and autumn comparisons include 2025, while approved annual-fraction references exclude 2025 because its winter contains provisional values.

A near-reference annual mean can conceal opposing seasonal changes. Use the actual month or season surrounding an observation when judging its hydrologic context, then retain the annual period separately for annual-model comparisons. These calculations describe observed discharge without assigning the seasonal contrast to a particular land-use or weather cause.

The record is six dated years, with fewer complete years at some gauges. Reference minima, medians and maxima describe those actual years and are not confidence intervals or long-term climatological normals. Gauge volumes retain their individual contributing areas and are not summed across nested stations. Streamflow context does not supply constituent concentrations or annual nitrogen, phosphorus or sediment export. Catchment boundaries, outlet/network position, source-period coverage and any documented transfers or regulation still need to match the intended scientific comparison.

For calibration-period selection, inspect the complete-period counts first, compare the intended target's mean/volume against its retained reference years, then inspect flow-duration curves and dated high-flow contributions. Stations with one or two complete years provide a narrow empirical comparison. Discharge at different network positions retains its own support; a shared date or station label does not establish catchment equivalence.

## Calculation contract

**daily_volume** — Original decimal daily mean discharge summed exactly before multiplication by 0.028316846592 m3/ft3 and 86400 nominal seconds/day.

**completeness** — Complete period volume requires every selected calendar date to contain a numeric original value. Observed-period volume retains only known days and is never annualized.

**flags** — A and e may coexist. Approved, provisional, estimated and revised flags are separate preserved source attributes; record flags include nonnumeric observations.

**seasons** — Winter is January, February and December of the same calendar year. Spring is March–May, summer June–August, autumn September–November. These disjoint month groups partition each complete calendar year.

**fractions** — Annual fractions require a complete numeric calendar year and positive annual volume. Monthly/seasonal profile means weight each eligible complete year equally; separately retained fully approved profiles use only years with A on every numeric day.

**duration** — Qp is the R-7 observed daily quantile at 1-p/100. Thus Q5 is P95 and Q95 is P05. Curves describe retained numeric days; incomplete years are explicitly labelled.

**thresholds** — High-flow tail includes days at or above the observed P95; low-flow tail includes days at or below P10. Ties may make those day fractions exceed 5% or 10%. Full dated original positions are retained.

**comparison** — Each selected-year comparison uses other complete calendar periods at the same gauge, independently of annual completeness, with separate complete-numeric and fully-approved reference choices. Mean ratios compare daily mean discharge; volume ratios also retain actual calendar length, including leap February. Annual-volume fraction comparisons use a separate complete-annual-year cohort. Missing target/reference support returns null.

**scope** — Gauge discharge is hydrologic context. It is not a nutrient/sediment load or model calibration fit. Gauge volumes are not summed across stations; upstream/downstream catchments and regulated or diverted flows retain their own identities.

Both comparison bases govern target and reference eligibility. Actual observed means/volumes remain visible even for an ineligible target, but a comparison ratio is null. Annual-volume fraction eligibility is checked separately. Monthly/seasonal reference flags join exactly from the corresponding period table by site, reference year and month/season; they are not duplicated as large nested objects in every comparison row.

Winter is explicitly January + February + December of one calendar year. It partitions that year's volume rather than defining a contiguous December-to-February event season or water year. Complete-year monthly fractions are averaged with equal weight per year; months retain their actual duration, including leap-year February.

## Rebuild and native report use

From the application root:

~~~sh
node observations/flow_seasonality_20261013/rebuild.mjs --app-root .
~~~

The rebuild verifies the frozen flow input and every original response/inventory body, reproduces the tables and report sections, and writes the native root manifest. It reconciles every annual count, source-flag count, mean and volume with the retained original annual aggregates before producing outputs. No network request is needed.

Import observed_flow_seasonality.sources.json in the existing Sources workflow. Its report_schema is upstate.observed-report/v1, so the ordered observed-report picker can compose it alongside the original lake, stream and calibration reports. Tabular outputs and original inputs remain ordinary report companions. This dated hydrologic context does not enter nutrient/sediment fitting as an annual mass observation.
