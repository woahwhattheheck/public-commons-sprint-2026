What the observations show

On 14 January 2026, the same monitoring project sampled SV-833 at 12:37 and SV-735 at 13:15. Across ten compatible characteristics, E. coli was 325.5 and 488.4 MPN/100mL, turbidity was 17 and 14 NTU, and phosphorus was 0.021 mg/L as P at both stations. Reconstructing total nitrogen from the same-activity unfiltered Kjeldahl nitrogen and nitrate + nitrite gives 0.44 and 0.55 mg/L as N.

The other 2026 station pairs have no shared sampling date in these retained responses. Their closest dates are four to seven days apart. Each station’s summaries therefore describe its own sampled activities; differences among those summaries combine location, season and sampling coverage.

The January pair is a 38-minute, same-day comparison. It supports a descriptive contrast between those samples. Attribution to a pollutant source, transport process, intervention or annual water-quality condition requires additional temporal and hydrologic evidence.

Sampling coverage comes first

The query window was 1 January–13 October 2026. The actual retained sample dates run from 7 January through 8 July. Empty months indicate no returned activity in this capture. The 100 equal repeat groups remain traceable by every original result ID and contribute one activity measurement each.

How the study was assembled

Twelve new agency CSV responses provide results, activities, limits and station metadata for RS-26250, SV-735 and SV-111. Six unchanged SV-833 source files supply its result groups, activities, limits and station catalogue. Original response bodies, public query URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 identifiers are retained in the input manifest.

Every selected result joins to its original activity and reporting-limit records by agency identifiers. Equal repeated values are checked across every original narrow-result column except ResultIdentifier and the identifier-specific limit URL. Their interval supports must also agree. This removes repeated weighting while preserving all 242 identities.

Below-limit results use nonnegative concentration support from zero to the original Lower Quantitation Limit, with an open upper endpoint. Means and medians are calculated from interval endpoints. Station summaries keep methods, units, fractions, speciation, result type, depth support and agency qualifiers separate. Same-day contrasts retain both activity times and monitoring projects. Nitrogen reconstruction adds compatible, same-activity unfiltered as-N components.

These agency observations provide measured context for watershed decisions. A concentration comparison alone does not calculate pollutant load: corresponding discharge and a justified temporal integration would be needed.
