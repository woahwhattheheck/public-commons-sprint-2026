The application runs from the workbench folder on your computer. A saved browser address can outlive that running session.

  1. Open the folder where you extracted the complete workbench ZIP.
  2. Double-click Open_Workbench.cmd on Windows or Open_Workbench.command on Mac. Let runtime preparation finish.
  3. The launcher opens the ready application at its current address. Select your original workspace and reopen the retained report or operation.

Keep the workbench terminal open while you use the application. Closing a browser tab does not close the workbench.
