## What the operator can do

Open the original Economic desk, choose a completed native report and a reporting zone, then use **Intervention quantities and sourced rates**. Choose a compatible original alternative and read its existing footprints. A footprint must already have been created by the maintained Scenario footprint preview. This read-only Economic endpoint never starts that preview, acquires source data, changes a model, or runs InVEST.

Start an estimate from the explicitly selected footprint. The complete original record, original footprint JSON, every file descriptor, native changed-area and changed-class-transition quantities, both complete rate catalogues and their exact source texts/hashes travel with the estimate. Reporting-zone area is context, never an implementation quantity.

Add the applicable items. Select a whole native changed area, a specific native transition, or a manual quantity with its actual measurement/design source and reason. Record applicability to the treatment, geography and source scope. Costs default to reference context until deliberately classified. Annual expenses require an explicit frequency and its basis. Assistance payments cannot become gross implementation costs.

The original 109-row catalogue remains unchanged. The separate ACES/Auburn 2024 southern forestry survey is a historical gross-cost benchmark, with overall and actually reported regional observations distinguished. Pine planting is not silently assigned to deciduous restoration. Its planting rates already include seedlings and oversight; explicitly review overlapping components. A source-rate override retains original rate, exact entered cents, a reason and its source. Incomplete entered text remains intact.

The quantity calculator uses exact rational decimal conversion and rounds each complete line once to cents. Capital and explicit annual totals feed the existing Economic worksheet; they do not change the physical response, original annual baseline, benefit timing or eligibility-bound assistance. The separately maintained dated lifecycle workflow owns those scenarios.
