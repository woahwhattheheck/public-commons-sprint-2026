**Export saved version** uses the saved comparison, including when working changes are displayed. Open the original task to obtain:

- **report.html** — the complete readable result;
- **observations.csv** — every original row and its decisions;
- **windows.csv** — the original analytical windows, measured and criterion units, support and source IDs;
- **interpretation.json** — the saved editable selection, reasoning and complete result;
- **monitoring-interpretation.zip** — all of those files, the full original study, the authoritative profile sources, reproduction module and opening guide.

Open the ZIP as a complete folder before following its local report/source links. The full scientific source archive retains every original result, including records outside the selected calculation.
