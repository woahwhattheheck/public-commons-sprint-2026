What the reporting limits resolve

Cyanide provides the clearest laboratory planning question. All twelve routine results are nondetects at 0.02 mg/L, equivalent to 20 µg/L. Their limits sit four times above the 5 µg/L reference in the agency's 2022 methodology. That interval includes values on both sides of the reference. A future cyanide scope should explicitly name the selected free/total analysis and request a reporting limit below the applicable decision value.

Cadmium illustrates the importance of retaining both sample fraction and rule vintage. The original assessment label is 0.4 µg/L, while every selected cadmium result has a 1 µg/L practical quantitation limit. The routine cohort includes 24 total results and 48 dissolved results, each spanning the same 24 dates. The latter are two original routine activities per date; they are not 48 independently sampled dates.

Original assessment and measurement vintages

The retained assessment unit is Big Creek, AU_ID 10475, BIMS index 3-10-3: source to the North Carolina–Georgia state line, C;Tr,ORW. Its 2022 overall label is Data Inconclusive, with 42 individual parameter assessments retained. The cyanide row is ASMT_ID 10673, Collection Year 2014, and literally says Cyanide (5 mg/L, AL, FW). The separate 2022 methodology states 5 µg/L. Both originals remain available.

These selected monitoring results end in 2012. The assessment's Collection Year field remains 2014 for cyanide and turbidity; the captured record does not map those assessment decisions to these exact result IDs. The join therefore supports source comparison and sampling design rather than a reconstruction of the agency's decision.

The current NCDEQ standards landing page identifies a June 2026 rule compilation. It also records the Class B primary-recreation indicator change to E. coli with EPA approval in May 2026. New field protocols should select the current jurisdiction, designated use and applicable rule edition. The historical source and methodology remain explicit in this report.

Mercury, turbidity and original quality comments

Routine total mercury has nine numerical results between 1.05 and 3.69 ng/L and fifteen nondetects at 1 ng/L, across 24 dates. The original 0.012 µg/L assessment label converts to 12 ng/L. The 1.28 ng/L result on 27 January 2012 carries Q2 for holding time after receipt by the laboratory; its field-blank companion also retains Q2. All original records remain downloadable.

Turbidity has 24 routine records: fifteen numerical results, eight nondetects at 1 NTU, and one Not Reported result. The maximum numerical routine value is 8.7 NTU. The 3 June 2011 result STORET-256221514 carries Z2, the publisher's data-reliability explanation, and has no assigned numerical value or detection limit. Two additional field replicates remain separate.

Missing temperature, dissolved-oxygen and pH records in the selected field-response body describe this exact query response. Broader historical monitoring, other stations or the complete source compiler may provide additional records.
