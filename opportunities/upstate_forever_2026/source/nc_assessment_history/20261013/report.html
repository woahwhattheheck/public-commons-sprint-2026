What the source shows

99 units retain the same label in all eight columns: 81 are always “No Data”, 16 always “Meeting Criteria”, and two always “Data Inconclusive”. The other 11 units account for all 15 adjacent-cycle changes.

The 2018 and 2020 totals are identical, but two unit-level labels change: Horsepasture River AU 10512 changes from “Data Inconclusive” to “Meeting Criteria”, while Trays Island Creek AU 13394 changes in the opposite direction. No unit changes between the original 2020 and 2022 columns.

These are assessment-label histories on a fixed 2022 cohort. “No Data” remains the publisher’s assessment label. The source does not establish a measured or causal water-quality trend, and it does not reconstruct earlier geography or legal classification.

Size and representation
The source assigns 105 units “FW Miles” and five units “FW Acres”. Their fixed 2022 totals are 198.443 miles and 692.791 acres, reported separately. The downloadable size table groups those same fixed sizes by each year’s original rating; it is not historical extent.
The 34 polygon records are alternate representations of assessment units already in this corpus. They add no units to this history. The separate parameter-assessment table’s CYCLE values remain distinct from these overall-rating columns.
