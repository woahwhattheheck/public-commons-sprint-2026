Bacteria: match the sampling design to the question

Horsepasture's 138 fecal-coliform records contain at most 2 samples in any 30-calendar-day window in this retained cohort. The referenced recreation criterion uses at least 5 consecutive samples in 30 days. The exported sampling-support analysis shows the actual dates and preserves censored records.

The 900 cfu/100 mL result on 25 August 2008 carries Q1: the publisher records that holding time was exceeded before the laboratory received the sample. The source value and warning remain together.

For a recreation assessment campaign, obtain the needed sequence of samples within 30 days with verified holding times. For thermal follow-up, cover July as well as August and use continuous temperature logging where duration and daily timing are decision-relevant. Pair water-quality sampling with an actual discharge measurement when the scientific question is mass export.
