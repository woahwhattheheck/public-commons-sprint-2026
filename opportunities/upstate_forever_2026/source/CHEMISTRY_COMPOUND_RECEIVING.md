## Scientific inputs and checks

The original 36 calibration records provide 18 paired reach/day positions. Two explicit measurement operators are retained at every position:

- Ammonium and nitrate nitrogen: `1 × ammonium_n + 1 × nitrate_n`.
- Weighted operator exercise: `0.5 × ammonium_n + 1.5 × nitrate_n`.

Both use the original elemental-N basis. Reference concentrations are exact weighted sums of the retained authored records. A combined record is held out if either original contributor was held out. Each new provenance record includes both original IDs and the original calibration run.

For this declared numerical exercise, the error scale is the sum of the absolute weighted original scales; selected records use an interval of plus/minus 0.2 of that scale with explicit endpoint flags. This policy does not infer source independence. The full derived input/mapping is saved before execution.

The course checks:

1. Native compound predictions and tangent rows against the weighted sum of their original single-pool calculations, then against central differences over the two original interior parameter values.
2. Recovery of the original authored fitted coefficients from mixed exact and interval compound records.
3. The actual day-zero reservoir inventory at included and excluded lower endpoints: membership differs while both distances to the interval closure equal zero.
4. Every interval's declared bounds, absent point value, membership and signed standardized residual; point scores and interval-support counts remain separate.
5. The new 512-draw compound ensemble against weighted combinations of every original saved draw, using the identical original seed, distributions and correlation. Original parameter draws must match exactly.
6. Native sensitivity and eight-candidate information design, original result paging and complete new observation-fit/interval-support CSV exports.
