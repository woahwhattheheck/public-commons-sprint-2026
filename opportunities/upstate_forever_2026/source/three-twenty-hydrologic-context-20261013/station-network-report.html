Geographic methods and exact source support
Geometry
Original whole NLDI reaches are retained untrimmed. Station locations are separate features.
Direction
DM source membership defines downstream scope; exact original endpoints determine a unique path from its origin reach.
Position
Hydrolocation is projected only onto its declared COMID, and only when the indexed WQP COMID agrees. A residual over two metres is reported as unresolved.
Distance
Approximate spherical geodesic lengths using radius 6,371,008.8 m; local tangent-plane segment projection with interpolated segment length. Distances are geographic network context, not travel time.
Measure
Original reachcode and measure are retained verbatim; measure is not substituted for a fraction of COMID geometry length.
Coordinates
Original agency station coordinates, NLDI indexed WQP coordinates and NLDI snapped hydrolocations remain separate.
