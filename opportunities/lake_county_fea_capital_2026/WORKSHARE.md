# FEA / Lake County engineering workshare

## Commercial status and division of authority

This is the engineering implementation supporting the original proposed FEA workshare for Lake County, Colorado's Capital Asset Management Plan. The proposal is **USD 28,800 fixed**, with **240 planning hours across eight active weeks**. It is not a client acceptance, award, invoice payment or confirmed start.

FEA retains the County relationship, prime contract, field assessment, appraisal methodology, professional judgment and final recommendations. The County and FEA supply and authorize the selected asset source, original financial model, relevant exports and review decisions. The software retains those authorities and their original source identities.

The workshare covers source preparation and reconciliation, one existing-model exchange, selected capital scenarios, decision records, a following annual source edition, staff handover and a complete independently reopenable application.

## Payment and schedule

The proposed invoices are:

1. **USD 8,640** at agreed kickoff.
2. **USD 11,520** at the agreed model/scenario delivery milestone.
3. **USD 8,640** at accepted commissioning and handover.

Invoices are due 15 days after issue under the proposed terms. The workshare does not assume County or prime prepayment, confirmed supplier availability or a funded source of payment. Review timing, available inputs, agreed model access and kickoff determine the actual calendar. Eight active weeks are not a promise of eight uninterrupted calendar weeks before those dependencies are established.

Two consolidated review rounds and two staff sessions of up to 90 minutes each are included. The delivery includes 30 days of correction for defects within the agreed delivered scope. An internal effort estimate does not convert an in-scope delivered defect into a new customer fee.

## Commissioning inputs and fixed-scope boundary

The initial selected engagement is one County source baseline, one agreed asset-register exchange and one existing financial-model exchange, with baseline plus two reviewed alternatives.

Before the fixed commitment, bind:

- Complete source-file inventory and actual row/file volumes.
- Record identity, ownership/maintenance/lease distinctions and property/room/site hierarchy.
- Original professional findings, methods, assessment/appraisal dates and authorized reviewers.
- Agreed workbook/export formats, with the actual writable ranges, output fields, guard formulas and original calculation/refresh environment documented.
- Original model period, price basis, currency and timing conventions.
- Actual recipient environment and full independent receiving criteria.
- Source corrections, exception/manual-review volumes and consolidated review participants.

The implementation supports complete CSV/JSON exchange and retains binary model originals. Direct spreadsheet/formula authoring, source-model repair, a new financial policy, field survey work, appraisal, production vendor integration, extra models or additional review cycles require an explicit scope decision. No private My Facility Plan or PubWorks endpoint is assumed.

Remote delivery is the pricing basis. Travel, purchased data, licensed systems, hosting and third-party charges are excluded unless priced and approved in the agreed scope.

## Public-source preparation and client delivery

Public documents and facility descriptions support a useful initial working edition. They can identify actual leased premises, County-maintained nonowned facilities, dated public budget accounts and original procurement requirements. They cannot substitute for the supplied asset register, field findings or the original model.

The initial working publication must remain labelled accordingly. A reviewed handover requires actual client/prime records and actual source/scenario review. Client approval stays a separate contractual decision.

The RFP's approximate asset counts are context, not invented asset rows. Funding requests and anticipated grants are not available capital cash. A source's stated total is not added to its component amounts. Lease costs preserve their original per-department/per-year unit.

## Intellectual property and contract treatment

The County's issued contract and work-product terms remain the prime's source authority. FEA should include any necessary preexisting-material, reusable-software and third-party/open-source exceptions in the proposal and resolve them before execution. This document does not unilaterally override the issuer's contract or waive any earned compensation or ownership claim.

Deliver the agreed project work product, full editable project sources and the applicable third-party licence notices. Retain the original software/licence schedule and any approved exceptions with the signed scope. The application source included here has its accompanying licence; source documents keep their original publisher rights and attribution.

## Buyer-facing review

Before an external product handover, open the exact archive being sent, run the received application, inspect every main screen at desktop and phone widths, verify the full report/downloads and review the shipped guides for the intended recipient. Record the actual result and screenshots. A native syntax check by itself is not a buyer-facing review.

No public-source preparation, local review or internal demonstration should be described as a completed County engagement, professional finding, client acceptance or paid commission.
