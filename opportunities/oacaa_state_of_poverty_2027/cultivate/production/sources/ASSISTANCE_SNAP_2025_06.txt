Public Assistance Monthly Statistics (PAMS) | Job and Family Services (ohio.gov) June 2025 

Key Terms 

Active Member – An individual receiving benefits 

Adult OWF – An individual 18 or older receiving Ohio Works First benefits or a minor who is a head of household and receiving Ohio Works First benefits 

Adult SNAP – An individual over age 22 receiving SNAP benefits 

Child OWF – An individual under age 18 receiving Ohio Works First benefits 

Child SNAP – An individual under age 22 who receives SNAP benefits and lives with a parent (natural, adopted or step) 

Children Authorized – The number of children eligible to receive Publicly Funded Child Care; children can be counted more than once if they are authorized to more than one provider 

Child Care Total Issuance – The total amount issued for Publicly Funded Child Care, including enhanced Step Up to Quality payments; this number is subject to revision 

Families Authorized – The number of families authorized to receive Publicly Funded Child Care 

OWF – Ohio Works First; the financial assistance portion of Ohio ’s Temporary Assistance to Needy Families program, which 

provides cash benefits to needy families for up to 36 months 

Publicly Funded Child Care – Financial assistance to eligible parents to help them with childcare costs while they engage in work, education, or job training 

Recipient – A person who received a benefit in the reporting month 

SNAP – Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program; the program formerly known as food stamps, which helps eligible low-income individuals stretch their food budgets and buy healthy food 

SNAP/OWF Individuals in Receipt – Individuals who received a benefit in the reporting month 

SNAP/OWF Households – The number of households that received a benefit in the reporting month 

SNAP/OWF Total Issuance – The total amount issued in benefits in each month, including any recouped overpayments 

Work Eligible Individual – An individual subject to the Ohio Works First work requirement June 2025
