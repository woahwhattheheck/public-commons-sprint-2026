L64:     Background
L65: 
L66:         Section 673(2) of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act (OBRA) of
L67:     1981 (42 U.S.C. 9902(2)) requires the Secretary of the Department of
L68:     Health and Human Services to update the poverty guidelines at least
L69:     annually, adjusting them on the basis of the Consumer Price Index for
L70:     All Urban Consumers (CPI-U). The poverty guidelines are used as an
L71:     eligibility criterion by Medicaid and a number of other Federal
L72:     programs. The poverty guidelines issued here are a simplified version
L73:     of the poverty thresholds that the Census Bureau uses to prepare its
L74:     estimates of the number of individuals and families in poverty.
L75:         As required by law, this update is accomplished by increasing the
L76:     latest published Census Bureau poverty thresholds by the relevant
L77:     percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers
L78:     (CPI-U). The guidelines in this 2024 notice reflect the 4.1 percent
L79:     price increase between calendar years 2022 and 2023. After this
L80:     inflation adjustment, the guidelines are rounded and adjusted to
L81:     standardize the differences between family sizes. In rare
L82:     circumstances, the rounding and standardizing adjustments in the
L83:     formula result in small decreases in the poverty guidelines for some
L84:     household sizes even when the inflation factor is not negative. In
L85:     cases where the year-to-year change in inflation is not negative and
L86:     the rounding and standardizing adjustments in the formula result in
L87:     reductions to the guidelines from the previous year for some household
L88:     sizes, the guidelines for the affected household sizes are fixed at the
L89:     prior year's guidelines. As in prior years, these 2024 guidelines are
L90:     roughly equal to the poverty thresholds for calendar year 2023 which
L91:     the Census Bureau expects to publish in final form in September 2024.
L92:         The poverty guidelines continue to be derived from the Census
L93:     Bureau's current official poverty thresholds; they are not derived from
L94:     the Census Bureau's Supplemental Poverty Measure (SPM).

L147:         Separate poverty guideline figures for Alaska and Hawaii reflect
L148:     Office of Economic Opportunity administrative practice beginning in the
L149:     1966-1970 period. (Note that the Census Bureau poverty thresholds--the
L150:     version of the poverty measure used for statistical purposes--have
L151:     never had separate figures for Alaska and Hawaii.) The poverty
L152:     guidelines are not defined for Puerto Rico or other outlying
L153:     jurisdictions. In cases in which a Federal program using the poverty
L154:     guidelines serves any of those jurisdictions, the Federal office that
L155:     administers the program is generally responsible for deciding whether
L156:     to use the contiguous-states-and-DC guidelines for those jurisdictions
L157:     or to follow some other procedure.
L158:         Due to confusing legislative language dating back to 1972, the
L159:     poverty guidelines sometimes have been mistakenly referred to as the
L160:     ``OMB'' (Office of Management and Budget) poverty guidelines or poverty
L161:     line. In fact, OMB has never issued the guidelines; the guidelines are
L162:     issued each year by the Department of Health and Human Services. The
L163:     poverty
L164:     [[Page 2963]]
L165: 
L166:     guidelines may be formally referenced as ``the poverty guidelines
L167:     updated periodically in the Federal Register by the U.S. Department of
L168:     Health and Human Services under the authority of 42 U.S.C. 9902(2).''
L169:         Some federal programs use a percentage multiple of the guidelines
L170:     (for example, 125 percent or 185 percent of the guidelines), as noted
L171:     in relevant authorizing legislation or program regulations. Non-Federal
L172:     organizations that use the poverty guidelines under their own authority
L173:     in non-federally-funded activities also may choose to use a percentage
L174:     multiple of the guidelines.
L175:         The poverty guidelines do not make a distinction between farm and
L176:     non-farm families, or between aged and non-aged units. (Only the Census
L177:     Bureau poverty thresholds have separate figures for aged and non-aged
L178:     one-person and two-person units.)
L179:         This notice does not provide definitions of such terms as
L180:     ``income'' or ``family'' as there is considerable variation of these
L181:     terms among programs that use the poverty guidelines. The legislation
L182:     or regulations governing each program define these terms and determine
L183:     how the program applies the poverty guidelines. In cases where
L184:     legislation or regulations do not establish these definitions, the
L185:     entity that administers or funds the program is responsible to define
L186:     such terms as ``income'' and ``family.'' Therefore, questions such as
L187:     net or gross income, counted or excluded income, or household size
L188:     should be directed to the entity that administers or funds the program.
