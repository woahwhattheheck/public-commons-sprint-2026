## Interpretation decisions

- The state one-year uninsured estimate is 782,626 / 6.7% for 2024. The separate 2020–2024 five-year estimate is 746,399 / 6.4%. Both use the civilian noninstitutionalized population and retain their actual denominators. They are not a year-to-year pair.
- June 2025 SNAP records 1,388,233 active members in 711,651 households. Reporting-office totals include the combined and unassigned rows. Programme adult/child definitions remain the printed PAMS definitions, rather than the under-18 universe used elsewhere.
- The Medicaid source was retrieved February 19, 2025; its enrollment month is unstated. Its population denominator is the ACS 2019–2023 five-year release. Expansion enrollment is contained within total enrollment.
- HEAP source counts are labeled 2024 applications. The source narrative calls them participating households. No approval, payment or unique-household interpretation is added.
- Both unemployment rates describe 2024: 4.3% in the April 2025 benchmark release and 4.4% in the revised May 2026 release. This is a revision to the same year.
- Programme receipt, applications, insurance status and poverty overlap. No total is produced by adding those populations, and receipt by itself is not used as an estimate of unmet eligibility or programme effect.
