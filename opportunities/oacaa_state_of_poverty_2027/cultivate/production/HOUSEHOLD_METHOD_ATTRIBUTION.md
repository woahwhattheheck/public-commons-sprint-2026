# Household-method source attribution

The retained source `sources/SSS2025_TechnicalBrief-public-text.txt` is the complete public-reader text representation of **The Self-Sufficiency Standard 2025 Update: Technical Brief**, by Sarah Brolliar, Annie Kucklick and Lisa Manzer, Center for Women’s Welfare, University of Washington School of Social Work, 2025.

Original: https://selfsufficiencystandard.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/SSS2025_TechnicalBrief.pdf

The source states on printed page 2 that it is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . That license and attribution continue to apply to the retained technical text and adaptations of it, independently of the repository’s general code license. The reader representation preserves extraction artifacts; consult the original PDF for its published layout.

The editable methodology dataset and explanatory guide paraphrase that source and organize its definitions for report production. They are adaptations, with source pages retained in every row. The concordance compares the original publicly issued 2026 Ohio report with those definitions; its descriptions are paraphrased. The report remains at https://oacaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-state-of-poverty-report_final-low.pdf .

Census data and their original source identities are covered separately by LONGITUDINAL_SOURCE_GUIDE.md and LONGITUDINAL_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json.
