COMMENCEMENT, INVOCATION, AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Mayor Gilbert called the meeting to order at 6:01 p.m.
A video recording of this meeting can be viewed on the Town’s Youtube Channel here.



ANNOUNCEMENTS AND PETITIONS BY THE GOVERNING BODY
During the announcements and petitions section, members of the governing body may share
updates on a variety of different topics or submit petitions for future consideration with the entire
board and the community. The following announcements were made:



   •   Mayor Jacques Gilbert recognized several former members of the Apex Board of
       Commissioners and Town Council who were in attendance including Dr. Doug Meckes, Ms.
       Christine Hilt, Mr. Carl Helton, Mr. Donald Grimes, and Mr. Wes Moyer.
   •   Councilmember Shane Reese recognized the passing of Mr. Lynn Council, honoring his
       bravery and courage.
   •   Councilmember Sue Mu recognized the hard work and dedication of the Apex Police
       Department in keeping the Community safe
   •   Other members of Council either had no additional comments or expressed gratitude for the
       strong community turnout and participation during the meeting.



ADOPTION OF THE MEETING AGENDA
A motion was made by Mayor Pro-Tempore Terry Mahaffey, seconded by Councilmember Arno
Zegerman to approve the meeting agenda as presented. Passed unanimously (5-0).



CONSENT AGENDA
The Town Clerk announced the following amendments to the consent agenda:
   •   Consent Item CN4, involving the Axon Inc. Aire DroneSense Program and Interview Room
       Camera System, was deferred to the Council’s Public Safety Committee for additional review.
   •   The motion for Consent Item CN23 was revised to clarify that Council’s action included a full
       replacement of Article V, Section 10 of the Town’s Personnel Policy related to Employment of
       Relatives and Nepotism.

      considered a proposed strategy to provide additional support and promotion for Downtown

      2026. Passed unanimously (5-0).

PH3   Rezoning Case No. 26CZ04 2913 Evans Road
      Council considered an annexation and associated rezoning request for property located at
      2913 Evans Road. The published agenda identified the requests as Annexation No. 828 and
      Rezoning Case No. 26CZ04.


      An initial motion to approve the request failed on a 2–3 vote, with Mayor Pro Tempore Terry
      Mahaffey and Councilmembers Arno Zegerman and Shane Reese voting in opposition.


      Later in the meeting, Council reconsidered the matter after the applicant offered an additional
      condition requiring the developer to dedicate public right-of-way along the Evans Road
      property frontage 30 feet from the roadway centerline, along with a 10-foot temporary
      construction easement for future curb and gutter and a five-foot sidewalk consistent with the
      minor collector street typical section.


      Following reconsideration, Council unanimously (5-0) approved the annexation and rezoning
      with the additional condition.
