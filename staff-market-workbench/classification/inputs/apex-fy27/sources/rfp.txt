Purpose

The purpose of this Request for Proposals (“RFP”) is to solicit proposals from qualified compensation
and classification consultant firms (hereinafter referred to as “Consultant”) to evaluate and recommend
updates to the classification and compensation structures applicable to the Town’s Police and Fire
positions. This would include reviewing the town’s existing classification methodology, job descriptions,
compensation structures, market position, pay practices, promotional differentials, specialty pay
practices, and implementation procedures. The selected Consultant will provide recommendations that
support recruitment, retention, internal equity, and effective administration of Police and Fire
Compensation .



Town Background
The Town of Apex is a rapidly growing and developing area located just west of Raleigh, the capital of
North Carolina. The Town was incorporated in 1873 and has an estimated population of
approximately 86,000 residents. The Town has approximately 740 FTE positions.

The Town maintains separate compensation structures for Police and Fire positions. These structures
were implemented in September of 2022. The current study will include sworn police (121 FTEs) and
fire (112 FTEs) classifications, as well as designated communications or 911 positions within the
project scope. The Town’s current compensation philosophy is to lead the applicable market by 5%,
on average.

Scope of Work

The Town of Apex seeks proposals from qualified compensation and classification consultants
specializing in local government compensation systems, specifically pay classification and structures for
police and fire departments. The selected Consultant must be independent and free from ownership or
control by any vendor, company or entity that may create a conflict of interest.

The proposing Consultant must demonstrate a minimum of seven (7) years of experience in creating job
classification structures, pay grade structures, and implementation plans to move existing employees
and future employees into the new structures.

The selected Consultant shall complete the following deliverables:

   •   Evaluate all covered Police and Fire positions and designated Communications/911 positions.
   •   Provide a detailed approach for developing a comprehensive understanding of all covered Police,
       Fire, and Communications/911 positions.
•   Review the duties, responsibilities, qualifications, working conditions, reporting relationships,
    and organizational structure of each covered position.
•   Review and evaluate all existing job descriptions for the covered positions.
•   Draft new job descriptions or revise existing job descriptions as needed.
•   Review the history and development of the current compensation structures for the covered
    positions.
•   Conduct an internal equity and comparative analysis of the covered positions to determine their
    relative value within the organization.
•   Assess the competitiveness and internal equity of current pay ranges and progression
    opportunities.
•   Review salary compression, promotional relationships, recruitment challenges, and retention
    risks.
•   Evaluate hiring and promotional salary practices, including credit for prior experience; make
    recommendations for calculation of appropriate starting salaries.
•   Recommend classification changes and market-based pay ranges, as appropriate.
•   Recommend the appropriate number and structure of pay grades or bands to reflect differences
    among the covered classifications.
•   Define recommended minimum, midpoint, and maximum rates; range spreads; midpoint
    progression between grades; and the relationship of the pay structure to the relevant labor
    market.
•   Review existing Town compensation policies and recommend revisions or new policies needed to
    support the recommended structures.
•   Develop a classification methodology, such as a point-factor system, for evaluating and
    reclassifying covered positions.
•   Develop employee placement models for any recommended revised pay structures.
•   Evaluate and provide recommendations on what specialized positions/assignments should be
    recognized for incentive pay; recommend appropriate policy revisions and compensation for
    each.
•   Provide an editable spreadsheet, database, or other tool that the Town may own, customize, and
    use for future classification and reclassification decisions.
•   Provide an editable spreadsheet or database to place current employees into the recommended
    structures while maintaining internal equity.
•   Estimate implementation costs and provide transition or phase-in options.
•   Ensure any new sworn police and fire pay structures are in compliance with existing Town pay
    policies or recommend detailed changes to policies or create new policies to supplement the
    structures if not.
•   Provide a new classification system (such as a point-factor methodology) for sworn police and
    fire positions, including an ongoing spreadsheet or database that the Town may own and
    customize and use to classify or reclassify sworn police and fire positions going forward.
•   Provide a detailed plan, spreadsheet or database to move existing employees into the new
    structures while protecting sound internal equity.
•   Prepare communication materials, including sample emails, presentations, frequently asked
    questions, and talking points for employees, supervisors, department leaders, and Town
       leadership.
   •   Provide guidance and support for questions, concerns from employees, difficulties utilizing the
       classification system, etc. for three (3) months after implementation of the newly created or
       revised sworn police and fire position pay structures.
   •   Provide all final reports, job descriptions, methodologies, databases, spreadsheets,
       communication materials, and other deliverables in editable electronic formats.

These deliverables shall be provided no later than March 15, 2027.
