# McGrath × Apex: editable classification and implementation workshare

## Concrete opportunity

The Town of Apex is procuring a Public Safety Classification and Compensation Plan. Its October 5, 2026 RFP requires an experienced public-sector compensation consultant and an editable tool the Town can use for future classification, reclassification and employee placement. All final materials are due March 15, 2027, followed by three months of implementation support.

Our proposed $4,800 technology receiving workshare adapts McGrath's own study outputs into a Town-owned editable operating workspace: agreed export intake, consultant-authored grade and placement decisions, three cost/handback iterations, deployment, training and three months of engineering support. The current product and public-source package already supply Apex's original FY27 pay data, authorized-position groups and exact RFP scope mapping. An expanded $19,200 custom engineering option is itemized separately below.

The [issuer listing](https://apexnc.org/bids.aspx?PRINT=YES&bidID=322) and [complete RFP](https://apexnc.org/DocumentCenter/View/56664) control the procurement details below. Page references are physical PDF pages.

## Why this fits McGrath's existing practice

McGrath's [company profile](https://www.mcgrathhumanresources.com/about/profile.php) describes 25 years of work and more than 640 projects across 41 states. Its [client list](https://www.mcgrathhumanresources.com/about/clients.php) includes Lawrence work involving point-factor development, police/fire compensation and incentive pay, Dodge City police/fire studies, and Holly Springs classification/compensation work.

The [McGrath brochure](https://www.mcgrathhumanresources.com/about/mcgrath-hr-brochure.pdf), page 3, describes its 360Comp Position Questionnaire. That is the proposed job-content collection route. This workshare consumes its authorized exports and attaches the resulting decisions, calculations and receiving material to the original study.

McGrath's [February 2025 Grafton proposal](https://www.villageofgraftonwi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/28985/Attach-McGrath-Proposal), physical pages 13–15, describes its existing point-factor, 360Comp and three-iteration costing process. Page 19 priced a 46-title classification/compensation study at $19,250, with benefits and travel itemized separately. That prior project informs our proposed downstream technology fit. The primary offer uses McGrath-authored professional decisions and supplies the editable delivery Apex requests.

The fit is our assessment from those published services and Apex's requirements. McGrath supplies its current staffing, references, project examples and proposal representations directly.
